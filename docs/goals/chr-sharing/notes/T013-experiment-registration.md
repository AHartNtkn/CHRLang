# Proposed experiment: conditional execution and local specialization

The next uncertainty requires executable evidence: do the specified conditional operations preserve CHR states and actually avoid repeated common work, at an acceptable bookkeeping cost? This registration defines a bounded correctness and operation-count experiment. It does not select a production architecture or claim representative runtime performance.

Status: preregistration for review, before implementation or measurement. Authoritative algorithms are [the conditional kernel](T011-conditional-kernel.md), [matching and wake-up](T012-matching-and-wakeup.md), and [scheduler](T012-scheduler.md). Record this registration's Git commit in every result manifest. Any subsequent change to hypotheses, workloads or analysis gets a new registration revision before collecting comparative results.

## Questions and claims

**E1, fidelity:** every candidate transition projects to permitted scalar transitions with the same explicit alternatives, fresh variables, constraint multiplicity and histories. Representation-only steps preserve projections. Failed contexts and quiescent answers match their declared observations. Maintain an explicit correspondence between scalar branch occurrences and candidate tickets through every split, failure and answer; check exactly-once publication before deduplication. Legal projected steps alone do not establish preservation of alternatives.

**E2, common work:** after a verified finite choice prefix, a chain of W choice-parametric expansions can execute W symbolic expansions across B alternatives, while independent execution needs B×W expansions. Matching, Boolean operations, scheduling and answer enumeration are counted separately. This claim is confined to the registered chain, not arbitrary programs or total O(W) runtime.

**E3, complete incremental discovery:** dependency-driven wake-up agrees with a complete scan on enabled tuples and matching witnesses at stable snapshots, including alias redirection and registration races.

**E4, local specialization:** for fragment R, bypassing general tuple search with a per-occurrence expansion controller preserves forward/backward use and eliminates the tuple arbitration it actually bypasses. It does not establish interaction-net efficiency.

These questions isolate correctness and mechanisms. C04, representative net benefit, is deliberately not claimed by this experiment. Elapsed time and memory are diagnostic observations; no architecture is adopted from them. A later performance registration needs an agreed workload rationale, production-appropriate implementation and predeclared net-benefit criterion.

## Exact semantic scope

Use a machine-readable first-order CHR IR, not a parser or user-facing syntax. Include simplification, simpagation, propagation, finite-tree equations in bodies, explicit body OR, conjunction, success/failure, fresh variables and nonground residual stores. Guards are limited to the always-true guard for this experiment; structural head matching still tests current equality without binding store variables. Additional pure guard implementations are outside the supported fragment, not silently accepted.

Queries contain initial user constraints and selected output roots. They do not settle O02 query-level equations/OR. Observe the entire finite residual store under the conservative equality criterion; do not design the answer UI or a resumable-answer API. Keep internal alternative occurrences until final recognized deduplication.

Use the scheduler's fixed committed policy for controls. For nonconfluent cases, additionally validate each transition against the legal scalar relation. Test driver scripts may select a permitted finite prefix to establish a shared starting state; record and validate every prefix transition. The setup driver may override the ordinary experimental policy only for this recorded prefix. Every control receives the corresponding projected state, and the fixed policy governs the measured continuation. This is a controlled experiment, not a language ordering guarantee.

## Implementations and controls

**S, scalar reference:** separate branch states, independent finite-tree unification, identified multiset occurrences, explicit histories and exhaustive tuple matching. Use a straightforward equation-worklist implementation that does not share the candidate's conditional matcher or wake-up code. Fresh namespaces are compared up to a bijection.

**P, storage-sharing control:** immutable/interned constructor storage and persistent branch snapshots with independent per-alternative execution. It shares the same scalar semantics and committed policy. This distinguishes storage sharing from executing common work once.

**C, conditional candidate:** immutable constructor nodes, condition-labelled bindings/memberships/histories, explicit frontier tickets, conditional worklists, matching witnesses and complete dependency subscriptions. Start with complete-scan mode, then enable incremental discovery as an ablation. Keep explicit per-leaf scheduling tickets; compact scheduling is not claimed. Resumable Unify and Apply use private/shadow work until validated source-step commit. Intermediate bindings, consumption and body goals cannot be read as committed state by another source step. Partial publication commits only the support whose entire scalar operation has completed; failure elsewhere cannot expose its partial bindings.

Use two exact support backends for C: extensional finite sets in bounded correctness cases, and reduced ordered binary decision diagrams for the condition-cost probe. The latter has monotonic dynamic choice IDs as its fixed variable order, a unique-node table and memoized Boolean Apply. Do not enable dynamic reordering in this experiment. Break Boolean Apply, reachability and tuple scans into resumable node/worklist visits.

For dynamic choices, frontier tickets are authoritative alternative identities. A ticket records its executed choice path; inactive labels do not enumerate more answers. Splitting a ticket under support P introduces a fresh decision d with arm supports P ∧ d and P ∧ ¬d, never globally active d. Inherited conditions remain independent of that variable. Verify that every state condition restricted to a current ticket path is constant. A nonconstant restriction signals an invalid representation or an incompletely recorded path, not permission to branch implicitly. The extensional backend replaces each inherited parent membership by both child memberships. The IR has binary OR only; any n-ary source example uses a recorded binary lowering, preserving one alternative per original arm rather than duplicating inactive choices.

Bryant's primary construction supports reduced ordered graph representation and Boolean operations, while explicitly warning of order sensitivity and exponential worst-case graph size. [Graph-Based Algorithms for Boolean Function Manipulation, §§2–4](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~bryant/pubdir/ieeetc86.pdf). It supplies no CHR sharing result. This is a direct primary-source retrieval on 2026-09-06, not a new survey or a claim that this support representation wins.

For E2, freeze grouping as follows: the oldest ready ticket initiates a batch over the finite snapshot of currently ready tickets, capped at 256. For Apply, include tickets with the same selected rule, ordered occurrence tuple and symbolic matching references. For Introduce, group the same shared pending-goal identity and symbolic term references, producing one new occurrence with the grouped birth/live support. This preserves common identity through successive chain steps. Other source goal kinds remain singleton operations in this registration; they cannot be grouped merely because their printed terms look alike. In these grouped operations, do not require equal projected values of an unexamined variable. Reserve and process the compatible support together, while charging discovery/reservation costs. Later arrivals start another batch. The registered prefix makes all B≤256 chain tickets ready before service, so one group is available at each chain step. Singleton-support scheduling is an explicit C ablation for the common-work grid; it is allowed to perform B×W expansions and is not claimed to satisfy E2. Report grouping as a scheduling factor.

For E4, use the same branch/storage services with either the general matcher or the specialized R controller. Compiler mode is an independent switch; do not compare different unifiers or schedulers and attribute their whole difference to specialization.

## Why this comparison does not select the conditional architecture

The conditional construction is executable enough for E1–E3; that is evidence of readiness to test it, not superiority over relation graphs or nets. The R-controller probe independently tests a source-property benefit common to several compilation approaches.

Named-superposition/memoized relation graphs remain included candidates. Their open question is a concrete CHR dependency and equality service, not whether the conditional experiment is fast. Ordinary/richer nets remain included, with their own finite encoding and administrative-cost obligation. A controller probe cannot satisfy that obligation.

Tabling and learned consequences remain included for declared logical solver fragments. Their exactness and dependency contracts are specified, but this experiment does not measure them. Copying with recomputation must be added before a claim about whole-search runtime; S is a correctness oracle and P an execution-sharing control, not a deliberately weak performance baseline.

No family is excluded because its prototype has not yet been built. E1 failure may reject or require repairing this particular candidate operation. E2 success may justify further investigation of this mechanism only. Neither outcome automatically advances or rejects another architecture.

## Workloads fixed before execution

**Required semantic cases:** all worked T008/T011/T012 traces, plus constructor clash, repeated-head equality, dynamic recursive choices, independent/correlated choices, duplicate occurrences, branch-private freshness, no-disjunction execution, disconnected pending failure, latent residual constraints, and wrong-version commit prevention. Include a forced interleaving where a binding changes between reading a dependency and installing its subscription.

**Application cases:** corrected unary addition in forward, missing-left, missing-right and bounded decomposition modes; subtraction as argument rearrangement; SK forward `S K K` behavior; a bounded ground SK synthesis case over the grammar T ::= s | k | a(T,T), with total tree-node size at most 7 and expected inclusion of `a(a(s,s),a(s,k))` for the supplied duplication behavior; and a separate backward constrained-hole case `a(a(k,D),H)` with D fixed to that duplication term and residual `no_c(H)`. The ground grammar has 102 terms; retain all matching results, not just the expected witness. The setup driver may establish this finite generator prefix before evaluation and must report that fact; it does not test unbounded synthesis efficiency. Preserve the source semantics in the translation and audit it independently. The full lambda notebook is a later application workload, not a prerequisite for E1–E4 and not claimed translated here.

**Generated correctness cases:** seeds 0–255, with fixed generator version and manifest. At most three rules, two head positions, eight initial occurrences, four constructor symbols of arity at most two, term depth at most three and two initial explicit choice sites. Recursion is allowed. Each generated case validates a prefix of 128 coupled driver rounds, stopping earlier at quiescence/failure or before a split would exceed 64 frontier tickets. These are intentional prefix bounds recorded in the manifest, not claims of termination. Timeout is an inconclusive resource result, not logical failure or a completed prefix. Validate transitions and reduce mismatches, rather than expecting every generated program to terminate.

**Common-work grid:** k in {0,1,2,4,8} binary choices, B=2^k frontier alternatives; W in {0,1,8,64,256} parametric continuation expansions; unrelated store sizes in {0,16,128}. Every initial shared state must have a recorded legal construction prefix. Report prefix cost separately from continuation and output cost. The measured chain is `carry(Tag,s(N)) <=> carry(Tag,N)`, starting with one shared `carry(Tag,s^W(z))` occurrence and no base-case cleanup rule. It performs exactly W expansions and leaves `carry(Tag,z)` residual. Tag carries the choice-dependent data without inspection. Count setup/introduction and extraction separately without excluding them from total work. Total: 75 parameter combinations per control/configuration.

**Adverse families:** immediate discrimination; alternating condition updates; alias paths of lengths {1,8,64}; two-headed joins with selectivity {all,one,none}; high history density; early/late failure; and completed support beside a divergent branch. Vary one factor at a time from the manifest's fixed base case before combining them. Freeze the exact generated IR and parameter manifest before comparative execution.

The source examples are application targets; the grids are deliberately synthetic mechanism probes. Neither is declared representative of all logic programs.

## Instrumentation and correctness oracle

Count source expansions, projected scalar steps, head-tuple candidates, structural node/pair visits, dereference visits, reachability work, Boolean-node visits/cache entries, subscriptions, wake-ups, stale commits, allocations, live/retained nodes, queued tickets, service actions and answer/dedup work. Count real events at their implementation boundary, not inferred savings from source syntax.

At each bounded checkpoint, project C under every current frontier ticket and compare its state to independently validated scalar transitions, accounting for conditional freshness. Compare the incremental engine's enabled tuples/witnesses with a complete scan on the same frozen snapshot after its relevant discovery notifications and subscription-registration rechecks finish. A stable store with queued notifications is not a completed incremental result. Preserve the failing IR, seed, trace, version and exact discrepancy. Minimize that counterexample before changing an algorithm.

For bounded terminating confluent cases, compare complete residual-answer multisets before deduplication and recognized answers afterward. For nonconfluent cases, compare the coupled schedule or individual legal transitions; never treat an arbitrary different permitted schedule as a bug. Finite fairness tests challenge the scheduler event rules; they do not prove general liveness from latency measurements.

## Execution environment and resource bounds

Proposed research implementation: Python using standard-library data structures and subprocess resource accounting. The inspected host reports Python 3.11.0rc1 on Linux x86_64. Record the executable path, full version, OS/kernel, CPU, available memory and implementation commit before running. If the interpreter changes, record a new environment and do not pool runs. This runtime is suitable for a correctness/operation-count probe; production speed claims are excluded.

Run one deterministic execution per semantic case/configuration/seed, plus one exact replay of every failure and each published representative trace. Count determinism is expected; unexplained count variation is investigated. The initial batch records per-run elapsed time solely for containment and diagnostics, without comparative timing summaries. Any later comparative timing report needs a new frozen batch with one warm-up and five fresh-process repeats, retaining all values and reporting median/range without architectural significance.

Limits: 512 MiB address space per child process, 60 seconds wall time per case, 200,000 logical source steps per generated case, 100,000 service actions per adversarial fairness case, and 30 minutes aggregate child-process wall time for the entire initial matrix specified above, including failure replays and counterexample reduction. Reduction has an additional 120-second per-failure ceiling inside that same allowance. Do not repartition the matrix into new batches to make incomplete results appear complete. A followup batch requires a new manifest identifying the prior incomplete result. These are containment limits, not language limits or acceptance thresholds. If the host cannot enforce a limit, stop before the batch and resolve the harness rather than silently omit it. Exhaustion and unrun cases remain explicit results; they cannot be counted as passes.

## Decision and stopping rules

Stop the affected experiment at the first reproducible semantic mismatch, missing wake-up, invalid condition partition, freshness/correlation error or premature answer. Preserve the evidence and repair the claimed algorithm or its scope before collecting comparative counts. A repair affecting hypotheses/workloads requires a registration revision.

E1/E3 pass only if all required terminating cases and all preregistered bounded-prefix checks complete within limits without mismatch, with the local invariant argument still applicable to the implementation. Completing a generated prefix establishes bounded fidelity only, not termination or completeness beyond that prefix. Finite testing remains evidence, not a general proof. E2 is supported only on completed grid cases where actual common expansion counts meet the stated W versus B×W relation; all bookkeeping counts accompany the claim. E4 is supported only where the exact source fragment and service behavior match. A competent general matcher may already avoid the same arbitration; zero additional savings is a valid result.

A resource ceiling or incomplete grid yields a bounded/inconclusive result with the failed dimensions identified. Extra repetitions or favorable workload selection cannot convert it to a pass. E2/E4 failure is a useful result; do not redefine a shared operation to hide repeated work.

After these questions are answered, stop this batch. The next review may authorize a cost-focused implementation, a net/service probe, a solver-reuse probe, or further correction. It must not promote the research harness into a production architecture by default.
