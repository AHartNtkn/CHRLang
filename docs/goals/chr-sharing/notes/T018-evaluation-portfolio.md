# Comparative evaluation portfolio

The research does not justify a winning architecture. It justifies a set of falsifiable comparisons. These are proposals for a subsequent implementation scope, not measurements or prototype authorization. T013 remains the detailed registration for conditional execution; the portfolio prevents that one registration from defining the whole project.

## Common semantic and measurement contract

Use the reference transition model and a recorded committed CHR policy. Compare explicit alternatives under that policy; do not interpret ordinary rule races as additional solutions. Validate substitutions, aliases, occurs failures, occurrence multiplicity, propagation history, residuals, source quiescence and final dedup. Every optimization needs a positive case and a nearby case where its eligibility fails.

For bounded comparisons, state all bounds and make source results directly comparable. For unbounded fair search, measure finite-answer progress without interpreting timeouts as failure. Report soundness, bounded coverage, throughput, memory and latency separately. A complete answer set can coincide while the implementation still fails the sharing objective.

Instrument actual source expansions, matching/guard work, equality work, support operations, history updates, cache/key construction, allocations/copies, compilation and output. Include W=0 and B=1 controls for common-work W and alternative count B. Vary unrelated store size so that a purported local optimization cannot hide a whole-store scan.

Do not compare a tuned specialized engine against a naïve control without recording the difference. Share competent indexing, immutable term representation and specialization where they apply equally. Report language-restricted candidates against their exact accepted subset and separately show reformulation costs for excluded programs.

## Mechanism comparisons

1. **Conditional execution:** scalar reference, competent storage-sharing control, conditional finite sets and a symbolic condition representation. T013 defines the exact initial semantic cases and parameter families. Hypothesis: common expansion savings survive the support/index/history overhead.

2. **Occurrence-local relation graphs:** the same closed relation workload executed through general matching versus provenance-based cached expansion, with contextual equality counted separately. Hypothesis: removing partner lookup and reusing expansion matters beyond storage sharing. Include repeated identical but distinct occurrences and external variable wake-ups.

3. **Named family services and backend integration:** compare the projection-preserving family service with encoded-machine execution and, if implemented, native HVM integration. Validate generated graph and label behavior for repeated values, independent recursive choices, binder duplication, failure outside the output path and finite service yields. Hypothesis: native distribution reduces work enough to pay for contextual services; do not presuppose it.

4. **Finite net services:** compile the same bounded source operations to controller/data nets and to a direct machine. Measure active-pair work, fan/eraser traffic, encoded-environment scans and request latency. Separately evaluate certified fresh/disjoint unification paths. Hypothesis: local rewriting and reuse compensate for service indirection.

5. **Delayed splitting:** compare early distribution, late distribution and bounded adaptive preference on both long opaque continuations and producers whose early choices prune speculation. Hypothesis: a policy can preserve sharing without creating unacceptable speculative work or starvation. Measure copied state and queued work, not just split count.

6. **Decomposition:** compare persistent certificates, conservative temporary regions with reunion, and no decomposition. Include ground multihead joins and late body introductions. Hypothesis: independent factor reuse outweighs dependency analysis and product caching. Measure latency of fair products and peak retained partial states.

7. **Schedulers:** use equivalent finite source services under per-alternative FIFO, sealed symbolic rounds and asynchronous sealed support jobs. Include an infinite recursive sibling, a huge finite equality/matcher operation and nested choices inside common work. Hypothesis: symbolic jobs reduce control overhead while asynchronous publication improves latency. Fairness is validated independently of speed.

8. **Structural solving and ECTAs:** compare direct residual constraints, exact operation memoization, regular membership plus explicit repeated-hole equations, and equality-constrained candidate spaces. Include finite constrained skeletons over infinite regular term domains. Hypothesis: correlated representation and intersection save search; track solver construction costs and partial answers.

9. **Whole-evaluator symbolic compilation:** compare bounded symbolic execution witnesses against direct execution with the same transition/state bounds and policy. Decode nonground endpoints, not only ground candidate programs. Hypothesis: global factorization and propagation reduce total solving work enough to offset compilation and increasing-bound reuse costs.

10. **Tabling, reconvergence and learning:** add exact keys, then sound generalization/certificates as separate variants. Include a repeated call with stronger caller constraints and states with equal outputs but different histories. Hypothesis: actual avoided work exceeds key/projection/provenance cost. Hits and learned-clause counts alone are not success metrics.

11. **Specialization:** compare finite unfolding and bounded residual variants using the same input/output information across engines. Hypothesis: reduced interpretation/matching offsets code growth and compilation. Track whether specialization duplicates a continuation that a runtime otherwise shares.

These comparisons need not all be large independent engines. A common source oracle and interchangeable service implementations can isolate mechanisms; complete candidate compositions are still required before a whole-system recommendation. A microbenchmark establishes one mechanism, not end-to-end synthesis performance.

## Application workloads and interpretation

Relational addition/subtraction validate equality and OR in multiple directions and provide a deliberately weak sharing case. SK forward evaluation, fixed-result backward synthesis and residual-hole answers exercise copying, filtering and potentially extensive shared work. The translated lambda relation exercises additional structure and norm/var joins. Add controlled workloads for joins/history, contextual cycles, exact duplicate answers and independent factors because the demos alone do not cover those obligations.

First establish semantic conformance. Then use controlled families to identify where each mechanism wins or loses, and finally test combinations on the application workloads. A failure or regression is evidence about a hypothesis, not a reason to weaken the source contract or discard the control. Record resource limits and incomplete runs explicitly.

## Decisions enabled by results

Choose a production direction only after total costs and semantic tradeoffs are visible. The owner may prefer a smaller globally restricted engine, a general engine with inferred regions, or a hybrid, but research has not supplied the measurements needed to rank them. No arbitrary numeric speed threshold or mandatory architecture is introduced here. A subsequent implementation scope should select the comparisons with the greatest expected ability to distinguish those choices and preserve the remaining independent questions as explicit future experiments.
