# Equality-constrained program spaces

This direction concerns compact sets of candidate terms and exact structural restrictions. It can complement the active CHR evaluator; its term-set semantics does not itself implement committed resource rewriting.

## Primary evidence

Koppel et al., [Searching Entangled Program Spaces](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.07828), §§3–5, introduce equality-constrained tree automata (ECTAs). Transitions carry equalities between subterm paths. Enumeration delays constrained choices using shared variables and intersects their candidate spaces. Cyclic automata use least-fixed-point semantics for sets of finite terms. General cyclic constrained automata have undecidable emptiness; their tractable construction restricts constraints on recursion and separates a constrained finite prefix from unconstrained recursive structure. Section 4 also permits partially enumerated results with remaining unconstrained automaton nodes.

## Direct relevance to repeated holes

Let N denote finite SK terms generated by `k | s | a(N,N)`. The regular automaton for N is cyclic as a representation but denotes only finite trees. For `P=a(H,H), no_c(H)`, use a root a transition whose two children refer to N and whose path constraint equates child 0 with child 1. This represents exactly `{a(t,t) : t in N}`. A plain regular tree automaton cannot represent that language, as established in T016-exact-solver-projection.md; the equality constraint supplies the missing correlation.

The same construction represents a finite constructor skeleton with repeated named holes: introduce one candidate-space variable per hole, connect its occurrences by path equalities, and intersect the applicable regular languages. Different holes remain independent unless a source equation equates them. Sharing an automaton node alone does not mean its two uses select the same inhabitant; explicit equality provides that meaning.

## Three different uses

First, an exact structural solver can store such spaces internally while retaining the original residual CHR occurrences at the boundary. It must preserve the closed-region conditions from T015/T016. A nonempty domain is not permission to fire a constructor-headed CHR rule on an unbound variable. Entailed information and actual source bindings must remain distinct.

Second, a declared relational generator can enumerate a compact candidate space through explicit source choices. Every emitted candidate needs a corresponding source derivation, including constraints that reject it. An automaton's union node does not authorize implicit search among competing CHR rules. A compiler must establish that its represented alternatives came from explicit OR in the source or from an approved logically equivalent closed region.

Third, exact partial results can be presented using existing residual syntax where representable. The example above can return `a(H,H) {no_c(H)}` without enumerating H. An arbitrary remaining automaton language may lack a name in the ruleset. Exposing it as a new residual predicate would be a language/interface addition; silently returning H without its restriction would be unsound. Internal use needs no such surface change if the original residuals are retained.

## Limits for evaluator-based synthesis

The SK evaluator's reduction behavior is not a finite structural grammar merely because candidate programs have a grammar. A finite bound on program size still permits nonterminating evaluation. Encoding all programs that yield a requested result requires either a proved specialized relation representation or bounded evaluation certificates. A bounded derivation encoding can be increased fairly, but its results and costs must be reported as bounded search, with duplicate proofs handled separately from distinct programs.

ECTA denotations are sets of terms. They do not encode occurrence multiplicity, propagation history or distinct explicit derivations by default. If the engine uses a term-set result to resume execution, its state-equivalence obligation is stronger than equality of candidate languages. Keep proof provenance or restore the complete source continuation before committing effects. Distinct programs with equal evaluator results remain distinct answers when the program is selected as an output.

The finitely-constrained restriction is an interesting compiler-region property: infer or declare that the structural obligations lie in a finite skeleton, with unconstrained regular recursion below it. It should not be imposed on the whole language merely to gain a solver implementation. Repeated constraints generated by recursive evaluation can exceed the restriction; that does not invalidate ordinary finite-tree CHR execution.

## Disposition

ECTAs resolve a concrete representation gap for repeated holes and strengthen the structural-solver comparison beyond ordinary regular automata. They do not establish a complete SK/lambda synthesis backend. The relevant next empirical comparison is ECTA candidate-space intersection/enumeration versus explicit hole substitutions plus regular membership, accounting for retained residuals, intersection work, partial answers and evaluator pruning.

A full relational compilation remains analytical work if this representation is proposed as the main evaluator. The present justified candidate is narrower: a certified structural region and candidate-space service. Its adoption is optional; performance claims require implementation. Further source work on the operational rwlog translation remains independent and active.
