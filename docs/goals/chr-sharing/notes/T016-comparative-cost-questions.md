# What the next implementations would need to distinguish

The candidates now expose different units of reused work. A useful comparison must measure those units and their bookkeeping separately. A conditional-store experiment alone cannot rank the graph, net, solver and state-merging directions.

This is analytical cost accounting and an expansion of the evaluation questions. It does not authorize implementation or invent a production language, platform or numerical acceptance threshold.

## Common expansion after an early choice

Use the controlled carry-chain family from T013: B alternatives carry different bindings of Tag through W recursive steps that inspect only a common finite counter. A scalar or storage-only persistent execution performs B·W source expansions. A shared event construction can perform W expansions if inherited occurrence provenance and opaque argument references survive each choice.

That count excludes support selection, context lookups, fresh identity management, activation, wake-up and observation. A graph implementation that performs B commits or scans per shared expansion may still have work proportional to B·W outside its event counter. A net implementation may replace each expansion by several controller, fan and service interactions. These costs must appear beside the expansion count.

The expected discriminating result is therefore not simply “W expansions.” It is whether total work and retained representation track common events, or whether hidden per-alternative work dominates. W=0, B=1 and unrelated-store-size controls remain necessary. At least one implementation with competent storage sharing is needed to separate saved computation from saved copies.

## Branch-dependent equality and aliases

Vary the number of contextual bindings, alias-chain length, amount of shared constructor structure and position of an occurs failure. Compare independently solved projected equations, a context-partitioned equation worklist, and the encoded net service. Record dereference visits, membership/condition operations, reachability visits, private-state size and effect publication.

Some operations can succeed immediately by identical representative identity even when the variable's value is choice-dependent. Others necessarily distinguish constructors or detect a branch-local cycle. These cases prevent a blanket claim that every use of a superposed value must distribute, or that every equality can remain wholly shared.

The indexed implementation and association-list service are different engineering points. Do not use the list's linear lookup cost to conclude that all net implementations have that bound. Conversely, a hypothetical constant-time native lookup cannot be credited until its alias, context and synchronization responsibilities are implemented or separately justified.

## Joins, consumption and propagation

Use the same multiset semantics in full-CHR candidates and controls. Vary head arity, partner multiplicity, overlap of retained heads and consumed resources, and history acquired before versus after choice. Count candidate tuples, successful witnesses, stale/rejected candidates, token probes and conditional removals.

A relation-region candidate can bypass general joins only for predicates that satisfy its closure contract. Report such a call as eligible and report the cost of the surrounding general region separately. If a globally restricted language excludes the workload or requires a routing reformulation, compare that reformulation as a language tradeoff rather than assigning the excluded program a zero runtime.

## Repeated subproblems and learned failures

Use repeated equal calls with deliberately varied caller restrictions. Compare no table, exact operation memoization, exact logical summaries in a declared solver region, and learned inconsistency certificates. These reuse mechanisms should be measured separately from shared event identity: independently created calls may have no common provenance but still admit table reuse.

Record key construction, freshening, caller filtering, entailment/projection work, table size and hits that avoid actual computation. A generalized call that loses pruning can expand more work even with more hits. Distinct explicit source alternatives and different program terms remain distinct before permitted answer deduplication.

The no_c/name/norm certificates supply meaningful solver workloads. Formula projection and baseline residual replay have different contracts. Their results must be compared under their own declared observations, not mislabeled as identical languages.

## Separated states that become equal again

Construct branches that take different finite paths and later reach structurally identical complete states, including matching live tokens and aliases. Compare no merge against exact state canonicalization with retained lineage multiplicity. Also include nearly equal states differing in one propagation token, duplicate occurrence, alias or policy age.

Measure canonicalization work, memory saved and future transitions actually shared after a successful merge. The sufficient bisimulation criterion makes these measurements meaningful. The frequency of such states in the supplied synthesizers is unknown; a synthetic success case demonstrates mechanism capability, not representative benefit.

## Scheduling and first-answer behavior

Compare per-leaf tickets, sealed symbolic rounds and asynchronous disjoint-support jobs under one declared committed source policy where feasible. Record logical microsteps before each answer, response latency, support fragmentation, queue growth and work undertaken in divergent siblings. Exact queue policy is part of the experiment, not an implicit language guarantee.

A loop beside a finite answer tests starvation. A huge but finite operation beside a short answer separates eventual fairness from latency isolation. A source split inside a common job tests whether completed support can publish independently. A worker waiting for an unresolved binding tests whether the producer can still receive service.

A native superposition backend must expose its own reduction and collapse costs. Counting only source steps while a backend normalizes without yielding would miss the scheduling behavior the owner cares about.

## Arithmetic and synthesis as realism checks

Addition/subtraction in multiple modes establish basic relational capability. They do not by themselves establish useful cross-choice sharing. Preserve a mode with unknown arguments, repeated-variable queries, finite failures and infinite answer enumeration; a fixed forward specialization is insufficient.

For SK synthesis, retain the same evaluator relation for forward checking and backward search. Include the duplication combinator behavior D x y = x y y, constrained holes, repeated holes, and further-answer enumeration. Count how much work each mechanism can reuse rather than assuming synthesis automatically supplies common continuations or repeated table keys.

The lambda notebook supplies an additional realistic constraint interaction through norm, var and neq. Its exact rwlog translation and variable representation must be pinned and reviewed before timing. The logical predicate analysis in T015 does not constitute a full evaluator translation theorem.

## What requires measurement

No source or paper construction determines which mechanism wins overall. Material unknowns include event reuse frequency, condition size, equality cost, cache selectivity, graph/net administration, residual canonicalization and peak retained memory. These require concrete implementations and realistic workloads.

Analytical validation can still reject invalid comparisons before code: weaker observations, implicit search, hidden failures, lost aliases, uncharged valuation enumeration, unfair service units and ineligible source reformulations. A future M2 decision should select experiments across these open mechanisms rather than equate readiness of T013 with readiness to end research.
