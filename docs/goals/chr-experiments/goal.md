# CHR experimental investigation

Implement and run the investigation in [the sequence](../../experiments/sequence.md) and [coverage audit](../../experiments/coverage.md), extending both as results expose relevant questions. Work is authorized through implementation, validation, experiments, debugging and authoritative external research. The reference remains independent. Experimental semantic alternatives are labeled; adoption requires the owner.

Maintain reproducible registrations, controls, raw results, counterexamples and question dispositions. Preserve negative and inconclusive outcomes. Continue independent work around candidate-specific blockers. Ask only for a necessary concrete decision after preparing its evidence.

The goal ends only after all relevant questions have sufficiently investigated bounded conclusions or exact owner/unavailable-external dependencies. For closure identify plausible further investigations and explain why their likely decision value does not justify their cost. An unfinished prototype, failed run, timeout, administrative limit, initial-list completion or production readiness is not completion. Feasible materially useful implementation, analysis or research must continue.

The rolling execution record is state.yaml; the experimental sequence remains the question/dependency contract. GoalBuddy-specific subagent roles are not exposed by this runtime, so work proceeds through PM fallback. No visual board is needed for this repository-native investigation.
