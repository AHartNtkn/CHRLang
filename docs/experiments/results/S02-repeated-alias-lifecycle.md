## Timing uncertainty was investigated

One-update wall times shifted substantially despite identical work and allocation. The [calibration](../registrations/S02-repeated-alias-calibration.md) interleaves frozen old and new binaries without rebuilding. Median paired new/old ratios range from about 0.94 to 1.11, but individual ratios span both sides of one in every mode, sometimes widely. This does not identify a consistent binary-specific cause.

The corrected matrix records Linux process CPU time beside monotonic wall time. Fifteen of 420 ordinary timing processes have wall/CPU totals above 1.2; the maximum is about 2.06. Scheduling delay affects some samples, but CPU ranges remain broad. CPU time does not eliminate frequency, cache or allocator variability. Clock-call overhead matters in tiny phases; slightly sub-unit wall/CPU ratios are possible because the intervals differ.

For 128 requests, 32 updates and four queries, corrected CPU medians/ranges are **1.223 ms / 0.995–2.312 ms for Filtered** and **1.551 ms / 1.158–2.167 ms for Indexed**. Those exploratory samples do not select a timing winner. The [corrected summary](s02-filtered-wake-attribution/summary.json), [initial matrix](s02-repeated-alias-lifecycle/summary.json) and [calibration](s02-repeated-alias-lifecycle/calibration-summary.json) retain every control and contrary sample.
