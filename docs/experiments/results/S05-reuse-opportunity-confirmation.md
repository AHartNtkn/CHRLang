# Equivalent futures can be cheaper to reuse, within a limited regime

Compact continuation reuse has a confirmed advantage when many alternatives share substantial future work. It loses in most tested configurations, and checked exact-source elimination is faster throughout. These results support a conditional reuse mechanism, not a general architecture choice.

**Next investigate sustained answer delivery and retention.** The [selection review](S05-opportunity-next.md) compares that work with broader call reuse and integrated execution. Whole-state reuse now has favorable and adverse cost evidence; its long-lived owners and the wider call-level mechanism remain unanswered.

## The comparison and its limits

**All 7,942 registered processes completed successfully.** There are 80 source configurations, 11 build/mode controls, seven shuffled ordinary timing blocks, two allocation repetitions and 22 cancellation processes. All 880 allocation pairs replay exactly. The [independent audit](s05-reuse-opportunity-confirmation/audit.json) reconstructs commands and order, checks source/binary hashes and ownership restoration, and recomputes all 720 paired contrasts from raw receipts. No samples were excluded.

**The sources vary reuse opportunity, computation and carried data separately.** Exact, renamed, early/late temporary-resource history and distinct-future families use one/16 alternatives, countdown depth zero/32, payload depth zero/32, and token consumption absent/present. Each preparation serves two changed queries. The independent scalar gate preserves complete raw answers, multiplicity and resource outcomes over 240 source configurations. The preceding work diagnostics establish actual reuse and contraction, rather than assuming them from mode names.

**Primary timing includes preparation, query setup, complete observation and disposal.** Source/input-inclusive times and individual phases are in the [summary](s05-reuse-opportunity-confirmation/summary.json). Native compilation is excluded. Allocation uses a separate build and measures requested heap traffic and growth, not RSS. First-observation timings are accompanied by setup costs, including eager answer construction. Two short queries do not establish sustainable memory or compilation-inclusive superiority.

## Which differences survived repetition

**Compact keys consistently improve the tested owned-key implementation.** They reduce requested allocation in all 80 configurations for both renamed and live-history keys. The registered timing criterion finds 60 gains for compact renamed keys and 58 for compact live-history keys, with no losses. The remaining comparisons are unresolved, not equivalent. All 180 diagnostic owned/compact pairs preserve the same reuse decisions.

**Reuse pays only in particular source regimes.** All 11 compact-live gains over direct execution occur at depth 32 with 16 alternatives: four exact, four renamed and three early-history cases. No distinct-future case produces such a gain. ## The feature-control shift does not decide the reuse result

**One of 80 feature controls has a classified slowdown.** It is the late-history source at depth 32, one alternative, zero payload and no token. Specialization's feature-on/off paired ratio is 1.228, with interval [1.113, 1.348]; median totals are 0.120 and 0.106 ms. Requested allocation increases by 272 bytes and peak growth by 152 bytes. The observed phase difference is concentrated in execution/observation, with smaller shifts elsewhere.

**The feature changes real machinery even when no recurrence is selected.** Current compiled source adds optional carrier storage, a carrier check in `Engine::step`, and insertion notifications. Those are plausible contributors; these data do not isolate their individual timing effects or rule out code-layout and run noise. The same-build contraction comparison is the mechanism contrast. Cross-build table/contraction comparisons are complete-build results and do not isolate contraction alone.

**Further attribution of that cell would not reverse its bounded reuse disposition.** Compact-live loses against both feature-off specialization and feature-on contraction by much larger margins: paired ratios 3.43 and 3.99. Contraction gains against same-build specialization. The sole feature shift is retained as uncertainty rather than treated as universal overhead or a reason to optimize carrier checks now. The other 79 feature comparisons remain unresolved, not proof that enabling the feature is free.

## Architectural meaning

**The representation penalty was consequential, but equivalent-future recognition still has a narrow demonstrated opportunity.** Compact keys preserve the previous semantic relation and substantially reduce costs. Their favorable high-reuse cases justify carrying reuse into later architecture comparisons. Sparse/distinct cases justify a serious recomputation alternative and investigation of retention policy; they do not justify rejecting call-level reuse or stronger sound projections.

**Eliminating this source remains stronger than interpreting it once and replaying transitions.** The exact-source control checks the complete rules and query shape, then directly constructs complete answers. Its source checking, setup and output costs are charged. It is hand-derived and cannot establish general compiler capability. Source-derived contraction demonstrates a narrower implemented analysis, including inspection costs and two short-case losses. Broader analysis, noncontractible useful work and language restrictions remain required.

**No whole-architecture or goal completion follows.** Call-level transport, broader relevance, eviction, sustained consumers, independent integrated execution, complete architecture composition and held-out challenge remain explicit obligations in the [governing sequence](../sequence.md). This bounded confirmation is complete; T075's broader investigation remains unfinished.
