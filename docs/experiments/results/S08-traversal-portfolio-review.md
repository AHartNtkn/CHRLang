**Select T072's useful interleaving comparison as the next bounded investigation.** Four packages since the previous review have measured coverage costs, separated matching overhead, repaired completed graph traversal and qualified its complete ownership. Reset the review count. The results constrain these mechanisms without answering whether a more integrated organization can avoid source work through partial information.

The latest [ownership gate](S08-traversal-ownership.md) provides favorable and adverse evidence: dependency allocation improves on continuing sources, template allocation worsens, flat sources expose overhead, and direct traffic remains lower. Those findings prevent unconditional memo adoption. They do not settle elapsed cost, richer graphs or integrated execution.
