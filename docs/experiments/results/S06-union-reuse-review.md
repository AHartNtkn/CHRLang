## Why this contrast comes first

The strongest ready distinct alternative is structural-prefix generated/access qualification followed by its cost comparison. Its repeated structural work is already demonstrated; actual generated execution and improved activation/partner ordering could either preserve or eliminate the apparent benefit. It remains required.

The selected union follow-through could invalidate the current favorable comparison at lower implementation cost. Existing finite denotation oracles, source fixtures, prefix traversal and lifecycle measurement already apply. A direct traversal checks whether any source alternative remains possible at each prefix, visits a complete assignment once and emits unique rows. This asks whether economical explicit execution can obtain the same benefit without a prepared union graph. It is a credibility check on the competitor, not refinement of union to protect a preferred result.

Output ownership is independently consequential. The measured retained-all difference persists after graph preparation is disposed. Compare an exact common set endpoint with a unique ordered-row endpoint whose eligibility is checked for both paths, charging any required sorting/deduplication or conversion.
