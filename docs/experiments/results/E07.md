# E07: bounded delay trades repeated work for speculation

All 930 registered runs and their full replay pass, with every non-time field reproduced exactly. Natural carry, doomed alternatives and finite refutation follow the preregistered work-count formulas. The experiment shows both a benefit from delayed distribution and a cost when early failure could avoid that work.

## Evidence and boundary

Freeze: `c9fdb80`. Registration: [E07](../registrations/E07.md). Raw results: [batch](E07-boxes.tsv), [replay](E07-boxes-replay.tsv). Build with `cargo build --release -p chr-boxes --example boxes_probe`; run `python3 research/chr-boxes/run_batch.py <output-path>` twice. Each case/quota uses a fresh release process and 60-second process bound. Elapsed time is diagnostic. Workspace tests, Clippy with warnings denied and formatting pass; four new behavior tests cover the registry under finite quotas, opaque lifting, ground resource interaction and bounded/unbounded refutation.

This first probe permits only a certified isolated, read-only, single-occurrence reduction to commute ahead of a pure equation/failure choice and suffix. General CHR executes normally outside that boundary. The E01 term/map/equality code is explicitly shared experimental infrastructure; the reference remains independent. Delayed child execution, promotion and adaptive preference are not implemented by this first probe.

## Successful common work

At k=8, W=256, noise=128, quota zero performs 65,791 applications. Quota eight performs 51,447, including 2,040 lifted applications distributed across intermediate branches. Quota 256 performs 511 applications: 255 branch-local choice preparations and 256 common carry reductions. Its 1,925 state steps compare with 132,485 for quota zero.

Requested allocation falls from 362,703,824 to 217,891,526 bytes. Peak live allocation above baseline remains exactly 211,597,176 bytes in both cases: retaining full residual answers still dominates this workload. Physical work savings alone do not imply output-memory savings.

The event count differs from E03/E04's 264 expansions because this prototype does not share choice-preparation applications. Its smaller requested payload than those engines on this workload also includes E01's interned terms and persistent indexed storage. The within-engine quota comparison isolates preference; a cross-engine ranking would mix these services and eligibility boundaries.

## Opposing workload and progress

With W=256 and 128 unrelated occurrences, both doomed alternatives fail. Quota zero performs one application and requests 757,620 allocation bytes. Quota 256 performs 257 applications and requests 1,148,588 bytes. Every one of its 256 lifted applications is unnecessary for this refutation. Peak increment is unchanged at 491,193 bytes. This is contrary evidence to an unconditional preference for delay.

The looping common producer has a finite source refutation because both explicit arms fail. Quota zero finishes in six steps; quotas eight and 256 finish in 22 and 518 steps. The Unlimited adverse control spends all 2,000 steps without servicing either failure, after 999 lifted applications. That observation is registered as unfinished; it does not establish a divergent source meaning. It supports retaining a finite bound on this preference, not a wall-clock fairness theorem for whole atomic services.

The p/q test prevents lifting when a common ground resource can later be consumed after a branch-local binding. All full residual observations remain correct. It demonstrates why checking only variable writes would be insufficient. Constraint-posting arms are also a conservative barrier here, including the registered infinite-sibling finite-answer case.

## Disposition

The first ancestor-lifting probe is complete; E07 remains open. Nested child boxes, early child failure and promotion, wider resource-aware certificates, adaptive preference with held-out workloads, and resumable service costs remain feasible and potentially useful. The opposing cases justify investigating a policy rather than selecting a universal quota from the largest successful case. The current syntactic certificate's application coverage also needs broader investigation; it is not a proposed language restriction.

E08 independent factors is the next first probe. It does not depend on making delayed splitting more general or faster. No language or production architecture decision has been adopted.
