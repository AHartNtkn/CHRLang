## Why this experiment comes next

**Compact union could earn its preparation by avoiding repeated logical search.** The [single-traversal comparison](S06-unique-output.md) establishes that its current output allocation is identical to a simpler direct traversal in all 96 matched scenarios. Union adds preparation traffic and peak heap. Repeated overlapping queries nevertheless show a sampled runtime benefit with both output collectors. Confirming that tradeoff could change whether a prepared logical region belongs in a complete architecture; more query reuse cannot erase the measured allocation gap without a mechanism change.

**The next comparison can isolate that uncertainty without another implementation.** Reuse the qualified ordinary binary and independent complete-set checks. Register repetitions from the pilot variability before comparisons. Keep overlap, disjoint, redundant and single-alternative sources; low and repeated reuse; membership and full output; and both set and ordered collectors. Preserve the existing timing scope of retained-all consumers explicitly. Reuse applicable allocation evidence for immediate/window/all consumers, without extending a retained-all timing conclusion to the other lifetimes. Check clocks, full lifecycle phases and paired execution order before drawing practical conclusions.

**Demand copy/discovery repair is the strongest competing implementation experiment.** The confirmed post comparison provides a serious demand competitor in favorable regimes and a concrete miss/discovery liability in adverse ones. Removing candidate copies or avoiding repeated discovery could change its competitiveness. Existing optimistic subtraction bounds allocation only; it neither implements a replacement nor proves a runtime limit. This remains required. Compact confirmation comes first because the comparison is already isolated and could resolve a different complete-path choice, while the next demand repair still needs a causal runtime attribution or a separately justified algorithm.

**Integration and call reuse have older unfinished costs and potentially larger architectural reach.** Integrated eligibility still needs token-independent discovery and credible merge/precedence alternatives, followed by primary timing. Inert-call reuse still needs extraction/key/replay attribution and total timing against regeneration, then substantive successes and failures beyond shallow examples. Neither has an external blocker. Among the four older cost packages inspected in the [preceding review](S01-prefix-portfolio-review.md), inert-call ownership is the oldest; this remains visible rather than being reset by recent work. Compact confirmation takes one bounded package, after which these implementation and cost questions must compete again. Ease of running an experiment alone is not its justification.

**Further matching precision has narrower immediate reach.** Deep-prefix confirmation already establishes conditional gains and higher heap than the simple local control. Remaining intervals near the practical threshold can matter, but additional repetitions would refine the same bounded mechanism. Broader partner plans, subscriptions and a tested source-informed selection policy could change the work itself. Carry the current result into complete paths and retain those missing experiments.
