## Causal evidence and its limit

Finite ground16 completes in17,313 service ticks with16 births and394 support nodes. Ongoing ground16 needs149,427 ticks and has already created83 births and10,071 nodes. At ongoing answer32,465 births and322,971 nodes are retained; answer42 arrives after18,775,637 ticks with944 births and1,334,653 nodes. Source work is running well ahead of delivery.

`Observation` currently creates a frozen `Births::histories()` cursor for each newly completed region. The cursor traverses causal histories and copies full world vectors; only afterward does `ObserveState::Filter` test whether a world belongs to that region. Regions therefore repeat traversal of irrelevant histories. Execution continues while queued observations receive service, creating more births, supported records and future observations. The finite control eventually stops adding source work, allowing observers to catch up.

This identifies a concrete feedback mechanism and unnecessary traversal, corroborated by the work counts. It does not attribute all timing or retained bytes to history enumeration without an ablation. Support algebra, historical resource scanning and query-scoped retention also remain. Merely pruning birth variables is unsound in general: distinct histories may carry raw multiplicity even when their visible residuals agree.

## Decision and next experiment

T039 is complete at this bounded comparison and causal selection. T040 selects support-restricted history traversal in publication, with an independent correspondence gate before a newly frozen comparison. Enumerate exactly the frozen causal histories satisfying the certified region, while preserving raw multiplicity, inactive-birth conventions, order, resumability and finite-sibling progress. The implementation must not enumerate projected branch engines or change source semantics. Exhaustive small causal-guard/support truth tables and adverse frozen-prefix cases provide an independent oracle; a selective large-prefix witness must establish that irrelevant subtrees are not traversed.

This is more valuable now than the strongest narrow alternative, optional explicit lineage recording: the latter is semantically plausible but has only448 bytes of measured growth over the ongoing ground range, whereas conditional publication reaches the service cap with roughly193MB retained. Broad conditional reclamation is the strongest larger alternative, but needs a more invasive ownership/remapping contract across live continuations, pending jobs and observers. First eliminating demonstrated irrelevant publication work can determine how much run-ahead and retained-state growth remains before that design is selected. No backpressure policy or mandatory language restriction is adopted.

The current conditional organization is not supported as an economical general ongoing-stream engine by these results. Its finite opaque-sharing evidence remains bounded positive evidence. Specialized explicit execution is the usable control here, with lineage lifetime still open. Parallel granularity, mixed phases, actual compilation and eager-region eligibility retain their R07 dispositions; this result does not close the architecture goal.
