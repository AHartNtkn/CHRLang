## What the evidence supports

Use competent source execution with selective access and inferred specialization as the general-purpose control. [R01](R01-native-pilot.md) finds that activation/access can change chain lifecycle by far more than generic/generated dispatch, while indexes impose unproductive maintenance on ordinary build and low-yield repair. [Anchor-aware enumeration](R01-anchor-pilot.md) reduces active indexed chain candidates from 16,838 to 772 at n64. Thus neither universal indexing nor dispatch removal alone is an architecture recommendation. [R05 specialization](R05-single-head-lifecycle.md) preserves source scheduling, eliminates selected cursor/history work, lowers requested allocation in every registered cell, and has favorable lifecycle medians in 15/16 cells, with separated ranges in nine. Its checked whole-program head-use analysis needs no mandatory language restriction.

Retain direct conditional execution as a viable organization for correlated alternatives with shared physical work. [Corrected maintenance](R03-conditional-maintenance.md) gives 2.65–10.23-fold lifecycle advantages on the bounded opaque cases, but 1.09–1.94-fold ordinary overhead. Discrimination64 is inconclusive. Monotone resource liveness accounts for a major avoidable maintenance cost; support and publication are still real obligations. These comparisons use the ordinary explicit control. R05 strengthens that control in a separate session; the results cannot be composed into a new quantitative ranking.

Prefer native finite lowering within the exact, correspondence-checked finite-consistency fragment tested by [R04](R04-lifecycle-pilot.md). It has lower lifecycle in all 20 cold/reuse cells against source execution and both CNF encodings. This avoids substantial general occurrence/search machinery. Eligibility, relation normalization, support propagation, trail restoration and complete answer recovery remain. This does not establish a general CHR lowering or native compilation break-even. The Boolean solver's costs are evidence about these encodings and complete-enumeration endpoints, not a rejection of learning on larger instances.

Keep integrated constructor/equality execution as a bounded alternative, without selecting its expansion now. [R02](R02-congruence-witness-pilot.md) corrects redundant transitive congruence work but retains higher requested heap; nested n256 lifecycle is 11.134 ms versus dedicated indexed 9.383 ms. Batch n256's modest advantage is inconclusive. Broader search and guard interactions could change this balance, but there is no demonstrated boundary-elimination benefit large enough to prioritize that implementation ahead of lifetime evidence on both viable search paths.

## Selected investigation and alternatives

T039 first establishes an independent streaming semantic/lifetime gate and then prospectively registers a bounded comparison of current conditional and specialized explicit execution. Use a recursive explicit-OR generator whose delivered full answers are identical and constant-size, with a constant-size semantic continuation. Drop outputs immediately in the main control; retain them in a paired consumer control. A finite generator checks exhaustion and complete multiplicity. Sample fixed prefixes, account for requested live heap and traffic separately from ordinary-allocator latency, then cancel and dispose the engine. Inspect lineage, birth/support, indexes and completed history to distinguish required live state from historical retention. Explicit search's full lineage vectors also grow; it is not presumed to win.

The strongest immediately ready narrow alternative is a same-freeze output comparison against specialized explicit execution. Conditional output6's corrected median of about 0.639 ms and specialized output6's 0.727 ms come from different sessions and do not rank them. Resolving that cell would refine a small finite regime, whereas streaming can change whether either organization is usable for ongoing queries and what ownership design it requires. Streaming requires a runner and targeted diagnostics, not a new backend, so its expected decision value is higher.

A repeated coarse-region parallel pilot is the strongest broader alternative: [E16 sizing](E16-regions-pilot.md) contains both favorable balanced coarse service and unfavorable fine service. It remains valuable, but its present comparison is less directly applicable to both current search organizations. Mixed phases may change composition decisions but can confound consumer lifetime unless the selected boundary is first understood. Actual compilation requires isolating generated artifact construction and code growth from toolchain/linking costs; it remains necessary before claiming compilation amortization, but does not resolve today's retention uncertainty.

Direct distributed named choice needs a complete source/publication gate before ranking; it is not prioritized merely because a prototype exists. Constructor integration, maintained joins, graph observers, larger application matrices, net/trace tuning, generalized tables and certificates retain the question-specific dispositions above. None is rejected by absence from T039, and none is a mandatory implementation queue.
