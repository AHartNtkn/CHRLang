## Why this question now

Join experiments measure several ways to perform general matching, but they do not establish when the language or compiler can avoid that responsibility. The earlier S07 privacy/ground-entry work separates declarations from stronger optimization eligibility; it does not answer head multiplicity or overlap. Treating one property as evidence for all restrictions would repeat an unsupported generalization.

The strongest ready alternative is broader equality relevance: distinct canonical merges still cause broad invalidation. It remains consequential, but same-root detection and source elimination now supply measured controls for the large reset penalty. Language properties can change the accepted programs and eliminate whole responsibilities, making them more valuable to investigate next than another local scheduling or invalidation refinement. This is a prioritization judgment, not evidence against precise relevance tracking.

## Required source comparison

Begin with separate single-head and overlap properties; neither implies the other. Define overlap in terms of possible enabled applications under this language's actual matching, guards, multiplicity and source priority. Do not equate different constructor spellings with a proof of non-overlap when partial or unknown information can enable them later.

Compare ordinary execution exploiting inferred properties, checked optional declarations, and a required-property experimental interface. Connect every property to a real beneficiary that removes work or state. A declaration that changes only admission labels is insufficient. Keep one authoritative prepared source per check; linking an extension must re-establish any property it can invalidate.

Use accepted and rejected sources: unary consuming chains; competing unary rules; propagation history; genuinely atomic multi-head consumption; disjoint constructors and overlapping/repeated-variable patterns; late guards/bindings; and source extensions. Try realistic reformulations of excluded programs and compare their complete residual behavior, resource winner, progress and cost. A sequential rewrite that exposes intermediate resource claims is not automatically equivalent.

Establish independent source/correctness witnesses first, then register complete costs if a property removes consequential work. Charge inference/checking, preparation, changed queries, execution, observation and disposal. The existing unrestricted controls remain valid experimental competitors. No second production baseline or preferred language policy is assumed.
