# Allocation savings do not close the complete execution gap

**Vector incidence buckets retain their allocation advantage, but the compiled control is faster in every tested complete-lifecycle comparison.** The next consequential responsibility is execution, not another admission-only refinement.

The [registered experiment](../registrations/S02-incidence-source-timing.md) validates72 complete-source entry processes and runs300 ordinary-allocator timing processes after60 excluded warmups. Each process prepares rules, runs two changing queries, validates complete answers outside measured intervals and disposes producers, preparation and retained answers. No engine/allocation diagnostics are enabled in timing builds.

## A complete high-degree source now exercises the representation

Distinct unknowns appear repeatedly in edge columns and constructor children. Binding rules merge them onto one value; consuming rules emit one receipt for every edge occurrence. Constructor equality may coalesce facts, but it must not lose duplicate receipts. The entry gate crosses widths8/64/128, independent insertion/binding reversals, both incidence representations and full/bounded/compiled execution.

Every answer agrees with both an independent scalar evaluator and an analytically constructed complete receipt multiset. Successive queries change width and duplicate multiplicity while reusing rules. This extends the earlier direct-store challenge into source execution with a serious compiled competitor.

## What the pilot measures

| Comparison across12 cases | Favorable timing screens | Unresolved |

| Vector versus set, full settlement |2|10|
| Vector versus set, bounded settlement |3|9|
| Compiled versus vector, full settlement |12|0|

A favorable screen requires at least10% median improvement and all five paired blocks on the same side of equality. It is an exploratory screen, not a confirmed policy. No vector/set comparison screens as a loss. The cases include broad repair, admission cancellation and the earlier low-degree readiness family; they are not workload weights.

For broad width128 with reversed insertion and binding, medians across the two-query sessions show:

| Execution | Query setup | Execution/observation |
|---|---:|---:|

The registered endpoint also charges preparation, engine/preparation disposal and consumer disposal. Separate phase medians need not sum to the median total. Source construction and independent validation are outside this runner's endpoint; compiler costs are not isolated. Allocation timings are never substituted for ordinary timing.

## Why the next experiment changes responsibility

An optimistic sensitivity calculation sets both vector query-setup phases to zero while retaining all other measured costs. In the reversed width128 broad source, full relational execution still costs2.48–3.28 times the complete compiled lifecycle across paired blocks; bounded settlement remains6.20–13.35 times. This is an analytical bound on an unimplemented ideal, not an optimized engine result.

That bound means further admission-only speedups cannot reverse this witness. It also explains why confirming a small bucket-speed margin is currently less valuable than attributing execution. If a future policy hinges on the marginal vector/set timing, it still needs registered confirmation; no representation default is selected here.

The execution code exposes distinct responsibilities to investigate: equality steps repair incidence and invalidate candidate caches; application discovery repopulates those caches; readiness traverses pending equations and constructor connections. Their individual CPU contributions are not yet measured. The next diagnostic will separate them on these exact complete-source controls, including successful shared equality, early failure and broad consumption.

## Decision

Retain both bucket representations and the compiled control. Keep vector allocation savings as scoped evidence, and keep unresolved timing cells unresolved. T072 next attributes execution before selecting a repair or broader representation intervention. Graph memo attribution and direct solving remain credible alternatives and will be reconsidered at that result, followed by the full portfolio review. Package count is three; the research goal remains active.
