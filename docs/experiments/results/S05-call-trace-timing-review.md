# Portfolio review: compare call reuse with stronger execution

**Keep T075 active for a matched compiled-execution control.** Call traces now have
isolated and live-caller source gates, exact ownership and complete ordinary timing.
They win eleven repeated-query comparisons against Direct while retaining roughly
twice its peak memory. Before improving the cache further, determine whether those
gains survive a stronger existing recomputation engine.

| Ready investigation | Decision it could change | Priority judgment |
|---|---|---|
| Same-source compiled execution | Whether the eleven reuse gains reflect saved computation that remains valuable after better matching/execution | **Next.** `chr-compiled::PreparedRuleset` accepts source rules and exposes indexed search. Establish complete answers, delivery order and first-answer cancellation on the same caller sources, then qualify full ownership and interleaved timing. Add generated execution using the existing emitter where it changes the comparison. No new baseline engine is needed. |
| Bounded trace retention and recomputation | Whether repeated-call speed can coexist with economical sustained ownership | Strong alternative. Completed-key and unfinished-machine growth are measured. A policy needs changed-query revisits and cancellation, not just a smaller final table. The stronger control could first change whether that investment is architecturally valuable. |
| Sparse finite projection | Whether sparse tables avoid Cartesian elimination work | Still required under T076. Dense preparation has a concrete elimination bottleneck; complete costs remain adverse in its current regime. Retain the source/work witness and ordered-transport obligations. |
| Higher-priority observers and broader call effects | Whether a broader resumable boundary supports more programs | Still required. Private-prefix success is not general interleaving evidence or a language restriction. The additional protocol needs explicit interface changes and source observers. |
| Other integration, restoration, native and parallel organizations | Whether a different division of responsibilities improves coherent architectures | Preserve their independent obligations. Nothing in the serial reuse campaign resolves them. Reassess at the matched-control gate or any obstruction. |

The choice is based on decision value and cost: an existing stronger executor could
overturn or sharpen the current reuse conclusion with little new mechanism. It is
not presumed to win. This comparison also advances complete architecture evidence
more directly than another isolated cache adjustment.
