# Immutable closed subtrees in the occurs check

The occurs check now skips constructor subtrees that contain no syntactic variables. A regression directly verifies linear visits for the state-building fixture while preserving open-variable and indirect-cycle checks. This strengthens the shared finite-tree kernel; no lifecycle speedup has been measured for the change.

[T050](R03-state-preservation.md) recorded 133,376 occurs visits when building 512 cells, equally with one or 64 mostly-failing alternatives. Each binding to `cell(s^k(z),H,H)` scanned k+4 terms. The new check still visits the open cell, its closed child and both H references, but does not descend through the closed child's constructors.

The [implementation](../../../research/chr-persistent/src/terms.rs) consults `is_closed` after dereferencing each popped term. Interned nodes are immutable; closedness is established from their syntax when created, without using bindings. Therefore a closed subtree cannot contain the variable being checked under any trial binding or sibling branch. Open nodes still traverse their children and follow bindings through the existing visited-node check. Transactional unification and changed-variable notification remain unchanged.

The [new regressions](../../../research/chr-persistent/tests/closed_occurs.rs) first failed with visits `[5,68,656]` for batches of 1,8,32 cells. After the guard they report `[4,32,128]`. Counter-free builds report zero diagnostics while asserting the same equations and changed variables. The adverse test checks an alias-mediated cycle in one sibling after another sibling binds its open variable, then checks rollback of tentative bindings and notification records when a later pair component fails.

The wider workspace check identified an affected work-count expectation in the reuse failure suite: `X = pair(X, closed_chain)` now visits pair, the closed child and X, independent of closed-chain depth. Its expectation is now three visits per repetition. Its independent failure, complete-answer and logical-step checks still apply. Historical experiment receipts retain their frozen work counts; they are not current diagnostic expectations.

T051 remains active. [Fork-owner attribution](R03-fork-owner-design.md) is the next implementation gate. Any lifecycle comparison of this correction or a snapshot intervention needs prospective registration, current controls and full setup/disposal accounting. The exact visit reduction is not an elapsed-time claim.
