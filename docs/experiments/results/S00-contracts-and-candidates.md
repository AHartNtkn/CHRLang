# S00: comparison contracts and coherent architecture candidates

**Reference scheduling and branch counters must not become universal architecture requirements.** Source inspection and a six-case executable probe distinguish those policies from complete answer meaning. This permits fairer graph, solver and restoration experiments without relaxing nonbinding matching, resource identity, branch-local failure or truthful completion.

This is a contract audit and candidate design selection, not a performance ranking or a language adoption decision. It uses worktree `cfbab00` plus the registered probe committed with this receipt. The preceding goal turn made progress by establishing the renewed sequence; this turn adds source-grounded and executable evidence. No experiment process was observed live at entry; unrelated untracked research remains untouched.

## Contract ledger

Authority is the [project semantic model](../../goals/chr-sharing/notes/T008-reference-and-candidates.md), read together with [README execution boundaries](../../../README.md), [syntax](../../../crates/chr-syntax/src/lib.rs) and independent semantic witnesses. The model is the current investigative contract, not a conclusion that its every choice is the best possible language design.

| Requirement | Current semantic obligation | Implementation freedom and required check |
|---|---|---|
| Terms/equality | Finite trees, most-general relationships, constructor/arity clash and occurs failure | Trees, arenas, equivalence relations or graphs; compare full aliases, including unbound outputs. Rational trees require a separate language change |
| Heads and guards | Match without binding query variables; guards currently expose only equality entailment | Generated tests or relational queries may replace matching calls. A hypothetical var guard is outside this implemented fragment |
| Resources | Equal-valued occurrences remain distinct; each head tuple uses distinct resources; kept/consumed effects remain correct | No prescribed allocation number. Replay may map candidate identities injectively to source identities |
| Propagation | No repeated firing of the same ordered occurrence tuple within its history; inherited history remains effective after OR | Tables are not mandatory. A certificate may make token storage unnecessary without changing permitted firings |
| Source choice | Search comes from explicit OR, with correlated repeated uses and fresh dynamic births | A family of branches need not be physically enumerated. Alternative rule schedules must not become extra source choices |
| Source selection | Commit a permitted enabled transition; nonconfluent schedules can differ | FIFO pending work and declaration order are reference policy. Match policy for causal ablations; validate permitted paths separately for architecture comparisons |
| Partial equality | Successful source firings must have a valid correspondence; failure stays local | No compulsory complete-unifier call boundary. R02's positive-fragment serialization is soundness evidence, not completeness or progress proof |
| Publication | No active inconsistency or source obligation may invalidate a trusted answer | Completion may be local to support/region. Empty physical queue or ground selected output alone is insufficient |
| Observation | Conservative full output tuple and residual multiset, jointly modulo fresh-variable renaming | Graph answers and exact comparison are allowed. Projected logical observations need a separate correspondence contract |
| Multiplicity | Distinct successful source alternatives remain accounted for in raw comparisons; equal residual resources are not collapsed | Unique delivery can suppress duplicate complete answers. Report raw counts separately when the comparison requires them |
| Failure | Inconsistent alternatives cannot publish or affect live siblings | Failed leaf events and exact speculative traces are not automatically required observations. Preserve them only in a declared diagnostic contract; count actual speculative work |
| Progress | Budgets are not exhaustion; finite sibling service and finite service operations require evidence | No fixed microstep ratio or physical queue is prescribed. Branch fairness does not establish fairness among competing rules |
| Lifetime | Returned observations remain valid for their declared ownership lifetime | No requirement to retain the entire query, use a particular allocator or materialize answers as trees |

### How new gates use this ledger

A policy-matched ablation fixes application selection, output endpoint and cancellation. Its trace comparisons may be stronger than a whole-architecture contract, but the claim stays within that ablation.

An architectural comparison first chooses a source family with independently known complete behavior, or explicitly validates each candidate's permitted committed execution. Confluence cannot be assumed merely because two implementations agree. If permitted schedules differ in outcomes or termination, record that language/progress tradeoff; do not compute a speed ratio for different accomplished tasks.

Raw-success multiplicity, unique-answer delivery and failed-lineage reporting receive separate fields. A candidate that compresses histories must establish its interpretation, including inactive births, rather than report the number of physical objects as semantic multiplicity. The T059 lineage gate remains evidence about its declared contract; it does not prohibit a future failure representation with a different physical account.

## Four complete candidate designs to investigate

These are executable design hypotheses, not new defaults. They share syntax where convenient, not an obligatory engine interface. Existing reference algorithms remain independent.

### A. Compiled incremental execution with selectable state restoration

Prepare predicate/constructor discrimination and partner access from rules. Occurrence or binding changes activate source applications; generated tests or retained update-driven joins replace repeated generic discovery where profitable. Terms use immutable shared structure with branch-local binding state. A query chooses a coherent persistence, trailing or checkpoint/replay policy; it does not need to expose copied engine objects.

Consumption commits against current identities and history. Pending bodies and bindings must receive finite service. Explicit choices create logical continuations; the restoration policy owns suspended state. Completion establishes no enabled work. Exact observations can detach into owned data or retain a documented immutable lease. Query disposal releases indexes, continuations and caches; prepared plans can outlive queries.

**Distinctive hypothesis:** compiled/update-driven access plus cheap restoration can outperform sharing by making repeated work cheap. **Favorable witness:** small changes repeatedly enable selective joins. **Adverse witness:** high fanout and broad aliases invalidate most retained work; common expensive equality recurs across choices. **Owners:** S01, S04, S05, S08, S09. Current compiled code is a useful control, not a required final architecture.

### B. Integrated constructor relations and consuming execution

Prepare rule patterns as queries over constructor root/child relations and occurrence identities. Equality updates representatives and directly activates relevant joins or incidence rewrites. Consuming applications claim identified occurrences in the same substrate; consistency obligations outlive their producer. Positive deductions may enable work before complete equality stabilization when correspondence permits it.

Choices require context-local overlays, reversible updates or supported records; select that ownership explicitly. Propagation uses occurrence identity rather than value equality. Publication waits for the relevant consistency and source obligations, then extracts the most-general joint answer. Regions/contexts own incidence, repair work and retained descriptors and must release them safely after observation or cancellation.

**Distinctive hypothesis:** eliminating reconstruction and solver/application boundaries reduces total work or invariants. **Favorable witness:** alias merges expose constructor joins that directly enable consumption. **Adverse witness:** dense rekeying, mostly distinct constructors, and speculative work before a clash. **Owners:** S02, S07–S10. Flat relational joins and local incidence rewrites remain distinct until their costs and required state are compared; the corrected R02 implementation represents only one organization.

### C. Direct named-choice and contextual derivation graph

Compile source occurrences or rule invocations into graph nodes with explicit resource identity and causal choice provenance. Carry unknown conditional values without projecting every alternative. Demand for a constructor or repeated-variable relation refines only the affected context; memoized expansion can serve later-arriving contexts. Context-specific consuming claims and propagation prevent sharing an effect merely because payloads agree.

A birth protocol distinguishes repeated use from independent dynamic execution. Failure and pending work remain connected to their interpretation even when absent from selected output roots. Finite graph service and context-local completion permit sibling delivery. Observation enumerates reachable source histories or unique answers according to the declared endpoint, preserving aliases and residual resources. Ownership must reclaim unreachable events and supports without invalidating another context or delivered answer.

**Distinctive hypothesis:** direct graph discovery avoids both branch repetition and expensive recognition of equivalent whole states. **Favorable witness:** delayed contexts reuse substantive work before discrimination. **Adverse witness:** distinct structures, low reuse, conflicting resource effects and long-lived historical retention. **Owners:** S03, S05, S08–S10. Named value equality, cached expansion, pull-tabbing, and distributed effect ownership are not assumed equivalent; each outstanding distinction receives a gate before being merged. Native HVM is a substrate experiment, not the definition of this candidate.

### D. Direct compilation of a relation or derivation

Analyze source definitions and a declared semantic boundary to derive a direct recursive procedure, finite constraint relation, or compact term-space representation. Prepare constraints/code once, with explicit handling of known and unknown query arguments. Avoid interpreter transitions and general history where a correspondence certificate makes them unnecessary. Solver choices enumerate source interpretations only when that mapping is justified.

When code remains contextual, preserve the surrounding effects and required progress, or prove they commute. Failure and successful witnesses follow the source relation; bounded evaluation remains distinct from refutation. Exact extraction must preserve residual information and multiplicity. Prepared domains/native artifacts, per-query solving state and returned answers have separate lifetimes. A parallel decomposition, if used, must be valid for the relation rather than inherit an equation-message interface.

**Distinctive hypothesis:** remove more runtime machinery than optimizing its execution can save. **Favorable witness:** selective constraints prune large candidate spaces or known recursive structure compiles to direct computation. **Adverse witness:** overlapping unselective domains, little reuse, unknown contextual effects and expensive witness recovery. **Owners:** S06–S10. Finite native solving, symbolic trace encoding and general relation compilation are not equivalent designs.
