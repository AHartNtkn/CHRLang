## T072 entry must discriminate mechanisms

**Start with equality that enables a structural consuming rule.** Include shared compatible deductions, mostly distinct structures, a broad merge that changes relevant matches, late constructor information and contested resources. Give complete source outputs, aliases and residual occurrences an independent interpretation. Make useful interleaving observable: either it avoids work or it creates additional activation without benefit.

**Compare actual responsibilities before choosing an implementation.** Contrast the existing dedicated/contextual path, equality maintained by CHR constraints and rules, and a local port/incidence rewrite organization. State where constructor information lives, who owns an equality class and a consumable occurrence, how a merge discovers affected matches, and how a rule commits consumption. A renamed relation table or wrapper around the same equality service does not represent a different mechanism.

**Implement distinctions that survive analysis, or prove their relevant equivalence.** An equivalence must account for operational work and ownership, not merely expressive power. Define accepted programs, source correspondence, failure and scheduling scope before timing. Keep a strong ordinary compiled or exact-source control where it implements the same contract; do not infer general architectural rejection from an isolated service or a single contractible example.

Reconsider call-level reuse and sustained lifetime after this entry/source package. Register complete costs only after correctness and sizing. T072 becomes active; T074 and T075 remain pending for their required remainders.
