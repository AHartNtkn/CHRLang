# Portfolio review: test longer-lived complete paths next

Move to sustained ownership and preparation reuse in the credible complete paths. The matched compiler control leaves demand and generated execution close in time, while conditional execution can retain less peak memory. Four-query sessions cannot decide whether those tradeoffs persist with amortized preparation, longer ownership and different consumers.

This full review follows the mixed-source gate, complete lifecycle pilot, timing confirmation and matched-initialization control, including its allocation diagnosis. The four-package interval is complete; reset the review count. The goal remains active.

## Selected investigation and its concrete outcome

**Select T074: longer-lived prepared sessions and query/consumer ownership.** Start with the qualified original/counted mixed-source paths, retaining competent generated Global/Active and inferred scanning, conditional execution and demand policies. Compare repeated preparation with reuse across increasing numbers of genuinely changed queries. Include immediate release, a bounded answer window and retained answers; separate prepared owners, active query state, consumer output and allocator-resident memory.

Inspect and repair avoidable retention at the preparation boundary before measuring it as necessary architecture. In particular, static initialization currently retains a rules vector after engine preparation has already taken its own source. Transfer ownership where the query initializer no longer needs that data, and independently verify original versus changed preparation on the complete sources. Charge the repair's full cost and retain the measured implementation as a frozen control.

Then register bounded steady-state time, requested allocations and RSS trajectories with changing queries, cancellation/reuse and complete disposal. Use actual source/consumer lifetimes rather than a repeated timing loop with hidden resets. Extend continuing production and reclamation as a separate contrast where active graph state survives between observations; many short queries alone do not answer that question. Repair fixture/interface obstructions rather than using them to end the comparison.

The result must distinguish fixed preparation that amortizes, active state that is reclaimed, required consumer output and avoidable retained history. It must identify any throughput/memory crossover without inventing a workload weighting or adopting a routing policy from the test shapes.
