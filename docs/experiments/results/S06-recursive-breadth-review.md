# Breadth review: measure the new recursive operation before extending it

**Run a bounded complete-cost comparison of source-derived recursive updates next.** The mechanism now constructs useful results while eliminating intermediate selection and occurrence handling. Its frame, constructor and retention costs could erase those savings, so correctness alone cannot support an architectural recommendation.

Four packages have completed since the last breadth review: reusable-prefix source gate, lifecycle sizing and its reporting repair, paired confirmation, and the recursive mechanism gate including semantic probes. The probes remain part of that fourth package; they do not reset the count.

## Why one cost package is justified

The [new gate](S06-recursive-accumulator-gate.md) extends source-derived contraction beyond unchanged payloads. Unknown-input suspension and observable terminal effects survive. The compiler therefore has a concrete new way to perform required work, rather than only an eligibility sketch. A bounded cost comparison can determine whether avoiding source bookkeeping matters when constructor work and full observation remain.

The strongest ready nonlocal alternative is graph-export attribution in S08. Existing growing-output results identify repeated copying, and measuring a correction could change the direct-graph comparison. That remains required. The recursive comparison comes first because it tests whether a source-derived execution plan can avoid overhead without changing representation or output contract, using existing scalar and compiled controls. Both possible outcomes change the next step: a loss calls for consequential attribution or a different organization; a gain makes the plan a credible control for broader architectural comparisons.

Broader integrated equality and strategic-port mechanisms remain distinct open work in T072. General call/continuation reuse, checkpoint/reunion policies and connected parallelism likewise cannot be settled by this compiler. Their implementation cost is a scheduling consideration, not evidence against them. Do not extend the accepted recurrence again before reviewing the measured result against these alternatives.

Artifact lifetime under unexpected signatures is another ready compiler investigation. It could change retained-preparation economics, but it does not answer the cost of this newly demonstrated operation. Keep it assigned to T073/S08; known-signature results remain bounded.

## Required cost package

Use the existing ordinary carrier controls and allocation meter, extending them only where required by constructor updates. Include ordinary feature-off execution, ordinary specialized execution in the same feature build, and the derived recurrence. Preserve unchanged passthrough controls to detect shared overhead. Where an exact/direct source control is applicable, state its contract and include its full required construction and observation work.

Vary short and long prefixes, small and larger update expressions, ground/unknown/later-bound tails, single and multiple recursive occurrences, and terminal success/failure/choice with consuming effects. Reuse preparation across changed queries. Gate allocator restoration and cancellation before comparative timing; then register precise bounded sizing or confirmation, counting preparation, query setup, complete observation and disposal separately. Native compilation is excluded unless credibly isolated.

Do not equate fewer source selections with lower total cost. If constructor allocation, update frames or retained original arguments dominate, investigate that cause rather than assigning it to an intrinsic architectural limit. Avoid repeated refinement when another design could change the whole decision more substantially.
