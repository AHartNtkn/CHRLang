## Measurement scope and next decision

Primary builds disable engine and learning diagnostics and use ordinary allocation. Diagnostic builds separately meter requested heap and collect learning counters. Operational budgets remain. Prepared rules and caller preparation are reused across queries. Full owned answers survive preparation disposal and are validated before consumer disposal.

The endpoint remains internal complete owned answers. Reserved outer output/phase storage is excluded; independent validation is outside measured phases, so whole harness process time includes work unsuitable for an engine comparison. First-owned timestamps are internal observations. External publication, caller-stage cancellation, sustained consumers and broader source lowering remain required.

Proceed to a prospectively registered bounded timing pilot with recomputation, eager and covered-state learning. The current source/accounting gate makes that comparison ready. Direct consuming-resource derivations remain the strongest distinct alternative: they might eliminate more runtime machinery but still require a concrete source contract and independent implementation. One bounded timing package now can determine whether the demonstrated allocation/work opportunity repays checking, at materially lower entry cost. Review direct derivations and broader compilation after the pilot and any consequential attribution, before extending reuse or depth further.

A pilot gain requires adverse and reuse/lifetime challenges; a loss requires checking whether a correctable recognition or preparation cost could reverse it. Overlap or cutoffs require discriminating evidence. No result here resolves general conflict learning, direct resource derivations or the architecture goal.
