# Retained matching has conditional gains, with a strong simpler competitor

Confirmation establishes a substantial median timing benefit over local scanning on the deep sparse width-eight sources. Deep keyed sources favor intermediates over generated execution, but their additional benefit over local scanning remains near the practical threshold. No scenario establishes a 10% advantage over both controls under the registered analysis.

**Keep retained matching as a conditional candidate; do not select it as the default.** Its higher measured heap costs and adverse shallow/early-consumption cases remain. The [full portfolio review](S01-prefix-portfolio-review.md) selects unfinished demand lifecycle costs next, while preserving consequential matching uncertainty and broader join questions.

It describes this machine/run series, not all hardware or CHR programs. Every sample remains available; broad individual ranges are not hidden by median inference.

The practical criterion is a full interval below0.90 for a10% advantage, or above1.10 for a10% disadvantage. An interval within[0.90,1.10] supports practical similarity under this design. ## Adverse inputs and memory still constrain adoption

**Shallow sources strongly favor simpler execution.** At width eight/four retained queries, sparse depth-zero ratios are1.281–1.398 against local scan and3.378–3.730 against Active generated. Keyed depth-zero ratios are1.623–1.748 and2.057–2.200 respectively.

**Avoiding unused maintenance matters.** On early consumption at depth32, the ratio against local scan is1.645–1.797. Deep mismatch gives1.312–1.374 against local scan, even though intermediates beat generated execution there. A generated-only comparison would again credit retention for benefits available through simpler local discovery.

The unchanged [allocation pilot](S01-prefix-lifecycle.md) finds higher intermediate traffic and peak heap than local scan in all64 scenarios. For the deep keyed witness, intermediates request2,634,196 bytes and peak at354,204 above baseline, compared with local scan's2,417,972/314,908 and Active generated's1,535,729/224,512. Retained output bytes agree across executors; the difference belongs to engine/source handling rather than a different answer contract. These are requested heap quantities, not RSS.

## Measurement limits and architectural consequence

Five empty-clock calibrations have median costs15,15,12,15,15ns. Applying the registered rule to every retained sample makes17 of128 contrasts instrumentation-sensitive; they receive no timing classification. No sample is excluded to improve a result. The matrix completes in about50 seconds on the permitted CPU0, without a concurrent assistant-initiated build or benchmark.

An exploratory [chronological-half check](s01-prefix-confirmation/temporal-sensitivity.json) preserves the highlighted median patterns: sparse/local ratios0.765 and0.776; sparse/native0.886 and0.894; keyed/local0.898 and0.876; keyed/native0.694 and0.691. These two witnesses do not constitute a stationarity test.

The intervals cannot establish that executions are independent or that the host is stationary. Individual ratios include large excursions, all preserved. Confirmation narrows median uncertainty under the stated assumptions; it does not guarantee each run is faster, explain every excursion, or supply held-out source evidence.

Preparation, query setup, execution, single-answer observation and disposal count in complete totals. Compilation, process startup, unmeasured preflight/validation and harness buffers remain outside. Source scope remains deterministic kept-prefix/consumed-terminal matching with ground nested constructors. No claim extends to continuing lifetimes, dynamic rules, delayed binding, subscriptions, broader planning or mandatory language restrictions.

**The architectural consequence is conditional retention with an explicit simple competitor.** Deep repeated matching can repay maintained work in time while costing more heap. Its incremental benefit is not established at the10% threshold against both competing controls on the same scenario. Remaining magnitude uncertainty could matter when comparing complete paths, so it remains required work; more samples alone are not currently the best next portfolio investment. A source-informed selection policy would itself need measurement and contrary cases before adoption.
