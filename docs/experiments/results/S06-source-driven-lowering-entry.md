# Next investigation: derive lowering from source properties

**Test whether the direct controls' advantages survive a compiler that derives its transformation from source structure.** Exact-schema lowering beats contextual execution in all 48 configurations of the [confirmed comparison](S02-contextual-confirmation.md), and it was also stronger than demand execution on the [earlier regular sources](S03-demand-lifecycle.md). Neither result establishes eligibility beyond its schema.

## Why this comes next

Actual local pull-tab operations remain a strong ready alternative because they could change work distribution beyond suspended calls. Cross-context consequence reuse and local port rewrites could change equality ownership beyond the contextual maps. Both require distinct source correspondence and effects work.

Source-driven lowering is selected first because the repeated direct-control results expose a consequential missing language/architecture boundary: which source properties permit eliminating that execution? A derived transformation could change the responsibilities required of an architecture, while an exact-schema fast path cannot justify a general recommendation. This is a prioritization judgment based on bounded evidence, not a performance conclusion about the other alternatives.

The first package is a source/eligibility gate, with no comparative timing. Review selection again after the gate identifies its actual accepted programs and boundaries. Do not accumulate further special-case recognizers under a general compiler label.

## Concrete first work

Audit the existing finite-table compiler, recursive/carrier lowerings and native generated access before choosing an implementation. Identify a transformation that differs from executing a prepared rule plan or comparing an entire program with a known schema. Reuse sound existing certificates where they apply; preserve their counterexamples.

Target a source-derived combination of deterministic constructor/binding work, explicit finite alternatives and consuming resources. Establish the dependency and ownership conditions under which effects can be contracted or compiled directly. Include multiple rule/predicate names and variable renamings, changed constants and query shapes, and at least one program not generated by the current schemas. The transformation must follow the source, not a workload label.

Use independent complete source answers, raw multiplicity and joint residual aliases. Adverse near misses must cover an external observer of an intermediate occurrence, a shared consumable claimed by competing calls, a late binding that changes a match, fresh variables and an ongoing branch beside a finite result. Reject or execute unsupported sources explicitly; a checker rejection is not a language restriction or semantic impossibility proof.

Compare inferred eligibility with a checked source property only when the declaration supplies consequential information the inference cannot recover. Do not introduce mandatory restrictions or a durable multi-runtime architecture. Measure checking, preparation and boundary costs when a correct path exists; compilation needs its own credible accounting before any amortization claim.
