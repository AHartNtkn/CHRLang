Registered before timing. Use the exact primary binary qualified by the call-trace
ownership gate. Re-audit ownership and binary hashes first. Repeat all 576 cells
(144 scenarios × trace/Direct/memo/memo16) in ten shuffled blocks, seed 607095:
5,760 processes. Keep complete source/preparation/query/observation/disposal phase

alongside timing. The existing process bounds remain unchanged.

Before runs, measure three 10,000-sample clock calibrations using the existing
ordinary recognition clock-check binary, verified against its frozen manifest.

Compare trace/each-control ratios by block. Qualified gain: median <=0.90 and all
ten ratios <1. Qualified loss: median >=1.10 and all ten >1. Otherwise unresolved.
Keep contrary observations and all raw results; no selective retries.

Hypothesis: call-entry reuse can improve complete time on repeated substantive
calls while paying admission and transport costs on unique/small calls. Higher
peak ownership remains part of the decision. After timing, diagnose consequential
cases and review all 57 questions, including stronger generated controls, bounded
retention/recomputation, observer interleaving and sparse projection. Do not infer
a universal winner or count unmeasured compilation/startup as a benefit.
