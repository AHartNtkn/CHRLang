# Repair demand discovery next; retain broader integration

Investigate demand candidate discovery/copy repair next under T071. Demand already has confirmed favorable regimes against strong generated and activation controls, and a specific costly unsuccessful-search regime. A changed discovery or candidate representation could alter a credible complete competitor.

The [reserved-selection experiment](S02-reserved-selection.md) completes the selected integration trial through source correspondence, work, allocation and exploratory primary timing. Its intended benefit occurs: flat discovery falls from 91 to 21 and complete Scan time falls substantially. Small one-token competition supplies a real overhead case. The result does not justify either rejecting integration or indefinitely refining this protocol before testing another mechanism.

## Why not immediately repeat or extend this timing matrix

All 48 protocol-to-protocol comparisons clear the clock-signal rule, and the large flat savings accompany independently observed work elimination. Five samples leave near-threshold and adverse magnitudes uncertain. More repetitions could confirm those differences, but they would not decide whether this encoded protocol becomes competitive with the qualified ordinary/local controls: the observed complete costs remain much larger.

Allocation attribution also limits a narrow next repair. Even removing all execution allocation leaves 4.70×–8.73× the best ordinary/local complete allocation total in every tested scenario. Cheaper precedence or ancestry could still improve runtime and absolute memory, but an execution-only allocation improvement cannot close the complete traffic gap. A different source representation, more reuse or removal of encoding/setup/observation obligations remains consequential and needs its own comparison. No allocation argument supplies a runtime impossibility or rejects CHR-expressed integration in general.

A credible continuation of integration should therefore either exercise a missing benefit/capability or change those broader responsibilities. Persistent eligibility, flat/contextual organizations, strategic local rewrites, useful partial equality, fresh requests and general bodies remain individually accountable. Reservation's exclusive-token-consumer premise must be rechecked at broader source boundaries.

## The next demand experiment and its strongest alternatives

The [post confirmation](S03-post-confirmation.md) retains substantive demand gains and a large miss liability: 6.889 ms against generated Indexed's 1.216 ms in the cited size-32 original-order case. The [copy attribution](S03-candidate-copy.md) identifies 47,744 argument/environment copies across its four queries, mostly environments. It does not measure a remedy's runtime. This makes the copy/discovery boundary a concrete implementation target with relevant independent cases and strong controls already available.

Qualify a candidate path that borrows unchanged match information or delays copying until a successful extension, or avoids repeated candidate discovery when a sound dependency permits it. Preserve partial bindings, late aliases, consuming occurrence identity, failure and cancellation. Include successful matching and cheap/unique cases so extra bookkeeping is charged. Compare the changed mechanism with the current demand policies and the qualified generated/activation controls under full lifecycle accounting; do not turn an optimistic byte subtraction into a claimed result. Register exact comparisons after implementation and semantic qualification.

Broader call recognition is the strongest competing mechanism change: coarser operation keys or cheaper sound identities could change the large transition-level cost measured in the [call pilot](S05-inert-lifecycle.md). It requires fresh-result and invalidation qualification. Demand repair takes the next bounded package because confirmed whole-query favorable cases already make demand credible, while its adverse cost has a specific independently measured cause. This is an effort/decision-value judgment, not evidence that broader call reuse cannot pay.

Actual composed paths remain required for the surviving demand, local/integrated and lowering alternatives. Extend them when the changed mechanism alters a credible candidate, including adverse eligibility, output and sustained ownership.
