## Rules for updating status

A stage can be analytically resolved, semantically gated, registered, measured or still unresolved. Record the actual state and source receipt. “Lower priority” affects order only. It supplies no negative evidence and cannot discharge the stage.

When selecting work, compare its likely decision value with the strongest ready alternative. Preserve coverage when ordering changes. When a result is unfavorable, identify the responsible mechanism and distinguish a correct bounded loss from a defective implementation, insufficient certificate or unfinished run.

A final bounded decision needs contrary evidence and a sensitivity argument explaining why the remaining uncertainty cannot change it within its scope. Otherwise keep it open. Exact owner/external blockers must identify the missing choice or resource, while independent work continues.
