# Experimental sequence for the unanswered architecture questions

Investigate the distinct proposals that remain unanswered, then compare the complete architectures they enable. Reuse the existing baselines. The research remains open until consequential uncertainty has an evidence-backed disposition.

**The immediate order is restoration, relevant-state integration, demand capability, then compact solving beyond the current finite reductions.** Those studies finish consequential comparisons already within reach while bringing distinct mechanisms into the experiment. Subsequent rounds cover reuse, discovery, source elimination, graph organizations, sustained execution and parallel ownership. Whole-architecture and language comparisons accompany these rounds rather than waiting until the end.

This is a prospective sequence, not a claim that the experiments below have run. The [57-question map](question-to-experiment-map.md) names every reviewed question. The numbered specifications below state the required contrasts; the [mechanism ledger](remaining-investigations.md#distinctions-that-must-not-disappear-inside-a-stage) separates proposals within a question. This document owns execution order; [sequence.md](sequence.md) owns the governing experimental requirements.

For each distinct proposal, first identify its claimed saving and a source that exercises it; then implement and measure that operation unless an applicable impossibility or operational equivalence settles it.

Compilation is measured through changed user-program artifacts, not by assigning a whole workspace build to one mechanism. A short finite peak or cold worker result cannot discharge the sustained or reused case.

**Whole-architecture checks begin with existing qualified paths.** Audit the applicability of existing common-source and mixed-source results at the first boundary. Extend their source coverage only where the new mechanism changes the compared architecture. Include at least one interaction source and one adverse source outside the optimization's favorable regime. If an attractive mechanism cannot yet participate, name and execute the missing integration experiment; it remains unanswered in the architecture comparison.

## What every investigation must deliver

Use one short result record per distinct claim. Its opening should say what changed in the architectural choice and why; technical receipts follow. Include:

- **Promise and scope:** the work the mechanism should avoid, source semantics, and what the experiment could establish against the current leading candidate.
- **Credible trial:** executable mechanism, competent existing controls, independent answers, a favorable witness, an overhead case and a source just outside eligibility.
- **Total consequences:** timing, memory and lifecycle; responsibilities and invariants introduced or eliminated; language restrictions and their expressive cost.
- **Contrary result:** strongest observed objection, whether an avoidable implementation cost explains it, and the experiment or sensitivity bound needed to decide that question.
- **Disposition:** supported within a named regime, contradicted within a named regime, analytically settled under explicit premises, or still unanswered with a concrete next experiment.

No direction leaves the sequence merely because it is expensive to implement, loses in a neighboring prototype, or has not yet received time. Do not assign a numerical complexity score: explain ownership, invalidation, coordination, recovery and proof obligations, including interactions absent from simpler competitors. Do not invent workload weights to choose among conditional tradeoffs.

## Detailed experiments

### 1. Does integrated equality remove enough work? — T072

**Compare the actual responsibilities, not just equality kernels.** Use recomputation, exact-state caching and relevant-state caching as causal controls. Compare contextual equality, flat constructor relations, CHR-expressed merging and strategic local handle/port rewrites wherever their repair or ownership differs. Reuse existing source and cost evidence for already tested variants; explain any claimed equivalence before sharing a conclusion between organizations.

**Run the missing cost contrast on changing contexts.** Include repeated useful deductions, changed irrelevant bindings, changes that invalidate a deduction, unique shallow requests, broad merges and conflicting consumption. Retain the current single/shared/distinct/changed source families and their complete-answer checks. Measure key creation, lookup, replay and retained maps diagnostically, then compare counter-free complete lifecycles against recomputation, competent scanning and applicable source elimination. A lookup that allocates nothing still has a time cost.

**Test useful interleaving separately.** Construct early contradiction and early selective-match cases where partial equality actually avoids later work, plus cases where interleaving creates activity without saving work. Keep stable positive guards and state-inspecting guards under explicit different contracts. The result must identify a boundary or repeated operation that disappears, and the obligations that remain.

**Required result:** a bounded time–memory–complexity comparison and a direct disposition for each distinct organization. If key construction dominates, test a sound cheaper recognition mechanism when a measured sensitivity bound shows it could change the decision. If the cost is intrinsic under the stated representation, explain that argument. Neither outcome settles other integrated organizations.

### 2. Does demand execution pay once dependencies and effects count? — T071

**Carry qualified dependency results into the broader capability comparison.** Reuse applicable within-turn miss reuse on/off evidence under the existing successful-result policies; rerun only when changed behavior or unresolved costs could change the conclusion. Reuse known/delayed, hit/miss, original/reverse arrival and shallow/nested cases from the work screen. Include the successful chain that still performs substantial discovery; it prevents a favorable miss case from representing the entire executor. Compare complete answers with independent scalar expectations and the strongest applicable compiled and direct controls.

**Measure ownership before primary confirmation.** Reuse preparation across changing queries, cancel during unresolved dependencies, retain answers beyond engine disposal, and test immediate, windowed and retained-all consumers. Charge miss-table construction, clearing, successful-cache state, dependency validation and disposal. If a consequential cost prevents a credible trial, attribute that cost before expanding the matrix.

**Give missing capability its own experiments.** Add nonground passive body posts and writable resource-head dependencies separately. Exercise late bindings, recursive resource-mediated dependencies, dynamic choices, competing consumers and hidden failure. A correctness failure needs a causal diagnosis: a defective implementation can be repaired; a semantic obstruction must identify its premises. A supported subset does not establish general eligibility.

**Required result:** when demand execution's saved work survives total costs, where it incurs overhead, and which source programs remain unsupported. Carry its credible favorable cases into the whole-architecture comparison, including work that cannot be eliminated by the strongest applicable source transformation.

### 3. Can compact solving replace enumeration economically? — T076

**Separate four mechanisms:** reusable compiled formulas; symbolic union/inclusion; existential projection; and lazy structural intersection/reduction. Existing names, disequality and normal/neutral denotation gates remain evidence for their exact theories. Each new operation must have an independently stated denotation and independently enumerated finite witnesses before costs are compared.

**Extend connected hidden constraints under changing callers beyond the current reductions.** Carry forward the applicable finite-formula results. Compare rebuilding constraints, reusing a compiled formula, existing finite projection, prepared name solving and explicit enumeration with early rejection on the new substantive cases. Cross selective restrictions with redundant or unselective restrictions, membership queries with full output, and little reuse with sustained reuse. Then test overlapping symbolic alternatives where union can avoid enumeration; preserve correlations, aliases and the specified multiplicity.

**Charge both representation and solving.** Measure compilation, fresh caller transport, solving, projection, exact output, identity lifetime and disposal separately where credible. Use stable-size changing queries to distinguish live obligations from accumulating identity history. Extend beyond finite-name membership when structural operations are the proposed advantage; do not impose a finite alphabet on a theory that promises more general terms.

**Required result:** which compact operations avoid necessary enumeration, which merely move its cost, and which language premises permit that saving. The existing transport-allocation bound narrows a repair question; it supplies no runtime rejection or result for symbolic union.

### 4. Which search work should be preserved or recomputed? — T077

**Price qualified checkpointed replay as its own strategy.** Use the resident replay source gate and add any missing ownership/cancellation checks. Compare competent copying, persistent/COW state, undo, root replay and several prospectively chosen checkpoint intervals. Restore bindings, consumed occurrences, propagation history, fresh identities and pending work. Test failure and cancellation during restoration. An existing root-replay result cannot answer the checkpoint question.

**Cross restoration pressure with useful search work.** Vary sparse/dense edits, short/long common prefixes, narrow/wide frontiers and early/late failure independently. Add a finite answer beside continuing work to test service obligations. Measure switching, replay, first/full answers, retained state and disposal; do not require every organization to implement the current branch interface.

**Keep adaptation and independence separate.** Attribute consequential uncertainty in the existing adaptive pilot before selecting a policy. Compare fixed and observable-work splitting on shared work and immediate child failure. Test finer occurrence-level independence, delayed linking calls and repeated separation/reunion, including frequent reconnection where certification cannot pay.

**Required result:** restoration and scheduling tradeoffs under matched semantics, plus the cost and missed opportunities of independence checking. Freeze adaptive policies before challenge cases. No universal snapshot or splitting policy follows from one favorable search shape.

### 5. Can recognition and replay replace shared execution? — T075

**Give broader keys substantive work to reuse.** Compare exact state, renaming, sound caller/relevance projection and stable operation identities with recomputation. Use equivalent calls from different callers that return fresh variables or resource-sensitive results. Add near misses where a later binding, resource claim or observable residual makes ignored state relevant.

**Separate successful results, failed operations and failed futures.** Check validity across changed assumptions and related queries for each. An equality-operation key does not establish continuation equivalence; a within-turn miss does not establish cross-query failure learning. Keep raw alternatives and resource identity outside any recognition step that merges logically equivalent work.

**Vary reuse distance and retention.** Cross repeated substantive work with unique/trivial requests, bounded eviction with indefinite retention, and immediate consumers with retained windows. Charge extraction, validation, transport, invalidation and regeneration. Compare with the strongest applicable direct-sharing path, not only an expensive serialized key.

**Required result:** a measured crossover or bounded limitation for each reuse mechanism, with its validity responsibilities. Investigate a consequential representation cost before treating an implementation loss as evidence against reuse.

### 6. When should enabled work be maintained or discovered? — S01

**Test remaining intermediate joins rather than repeat only full-prefix retention.** Use weakly keyed many-to-many rules with a large reusable partial join and a small changing subset. Compare locally invalidated intermediate results, subscriptions, indexed rediscovery and scanning. Add broad invalidation, equal-key collisions, consuming updates and cheap one-shot keyed queries.

**Separate planning from code generation.** Compare source-order and selectivity-informed partner discovery, constructor discrimination, index selection and wake policies on many rules and changing key availability. Execute the same chosen plan as prepared data and generated code. Reuse the [existing compilation evidence](results/S06-compilation-inventory.md); measure a new regime only when it could change its bounded conclusion.

**Required result:** the maintenance/recomputation boundary and whether source information can identify it economically. Count preparation, invalidation and retained state. Source-derived omission of output-only matcher state must survive later rules or queries making that state relevant.

### 7. How much execution can source analysis eliminate? — T073

**Extend two independent paths:** direct consuming-resource derivations and recursive/contextual lowering. Surround otherwise eligible work with unknown inputs, guards, external observers and competing consumers. Compare direct solving, a credible learning solver, prepared execution and native generation only where each preserves the same contract. Retain ordinary execution at real unknown or effectful boundaries.

**Measure incremental learning as well as one-shot solving.** Use compatible changing constraints and substantive repeated failures, plus sparse successful work and invalidating assumptions. Separate relation construction, retained explanations, checking and output. A loss from encoding interpreter traces does not answer direct derivation or learning.

**Extend compilation economics through actual changed artifacts.** Reuse source plans where applicable, then vary program changes, code growth, query reuse and artifact retention. Count independent user-program generation, compilation, loading, runtime and disposal. Existing observed amortization remains a control, so the new trial must identify the additional source or lifetime question it answers.

**Required result:** accepted and rejected source boundaries, total-cost crossovers, and runtime responsibilities actually eliminated. Distinguish conservative analysis from intrinsic semantic ineligibility. A compiler is not selected merely because emitted execution is faster.

### 8. Do different choice representations change the architecture? — T071 / T074

**Compare compressed choice conditions with eager partitions and explicit search.** Use independent and correlated choices, dynamic choice births, delayed contexts and shared deductions. Test support construction, intersection and exact extraction before projection. Include mostly incompatible contexts and immediate discrimination where retaining common structure has little value.

**Preserve the distinctions among graph mechanisms.** Demand expansion, local pull-tabbing, reuse across fresh applications and strategic rewrites need separate source witnesses or a demonstrated equivalence covering ownership and discovery. Reuse the existing lifting and derivation results for their tested scope. A service encoded as a graph does not represent an executor that avoids that service boundary.

**Required result:** complete source work avoided after correlation, consumption, failure and output count. Carry a favorable representation into sustained ownership and complete-architecture tests. Fewer support operations alone cannot select it.

### 9. Do gains survive long execution and exact output? — T074 / S08

**Run trajectories rather than only finite peaks.** Use stable-size changing queries and ongoing streams, with immediate release, bounded retained windows and retained-all answers. Compare reclamation, bounded caching and regeneration. Identify prepared artifacts, engine history, identity tables and consumer-owned outputs separately; cancel and reuse preparation during the run.

**Test exact observation directly.** Compare eager trees, shared graph export, mapping-sensitive reuse and exact symmetry refinement on shared large outputs, distinct small answers and symmetric residuals. Include equal local fingerprints with unequal global correlation. Charge export and exact comparison jointly when they share work; a fingerprint cannot certify an answer.

**Test publication under pressure.** Compare bounded quotas/backpressure with existing schedules using duplicate-heavy output, skewed work and a finite sibling beside continuing computation. Record both source progress and delivery. A cutoff is an unfinished endpoint, not a completed runtime or failed answer.

**Required result:** sustainable engine memory under releasing consumers, unavoidable retention under retaining consumers, and latency/throughput tradeoffs under an explicit progress contract. A short-run gain needs this follow-through before supporting an architectural recommendation.

### 10. Can native or parallel ownership repay coordination? — T080 / S09

**Qualify native local effects before scaling them.** Extend actual claim/commit/publication through structured terms, dynamic choices, failed branches and cancellation between claim and publication. Compare central and distributed ownership on disjoint and contested occurrences. Independently check complete effects and correspondence; constructor wrappers do not establish interruptibility.

**Run the independent-worker comparison separately.** Compare serial, one worker and reused multiple workers over shared immutable preparation. Include cold starts, changed queries, balanced/skewed work, useful work surviving serial lowering, small tasks and output-product pressure. Count transfer, synchronization, abandoned work, cancellation and shutdown. Establish available cores and affinity before choosing worker counts.

**Required result:** a granularity/scaling boundary for independent work and a separate result for connected ownership. If one substrate cannot support the source contract, assess the strongest feasible substrate for the abstract mechanism. Hardware limitations bound the experiment; they do not reject a different organization.

### 11. What language properties are worth their cost? — T079 / S07

**Attach a language comparison to each executable beneficiary.** For modes, groundness, ownership/linearity, nonoverlap, finite domains and progress properties, compare inference, checked declarations and mandatory restrictions wherever their consequences differ. Supply an accepted program, a near miss, a realistic reformulation or exclusion, and a boundary with unrestricted code.

**State semantic alternatives explicitly.** Compare stable and state-inspecting guards; exact and projected observations; logical sets and consuming multisets. Show changed aliases, duplicate resources, residuals, failures and progress where relevant. Do not label a different answer contract an optimization of the original one.

**Required result:** checking and boundary costs, missed optimization opportunities, runtime obligations removed and expressive consequences. Experiments establish those consequences; adopting a consequential language contract remains a user decision. No adoption question is needed merely to run both explicitly specified alternatives.

### 12. Which complete architecture earns its complexity? — T078 / S10

**Start once two credible complete paths support a shared source family.** Compare conventional execution, surviving integrated/demand organizations and direct solving/lowering where applicable. Include a serious replacement for the leading organization. Every proposed combination must face a simpler complete competitor.

**Use interactions that component studies cannot decide.** Combine joins, aliases and choices; shared deductions, consuming failure and exact output; or lowering, unknown inputs and external effects. Include sources outside optimized eligibility, preparation reuse and sustained ownership. Charge routing, conversions, duplicated state, compilation and disposal instead of adding isolated component timings.

**Required result:** conditional whole-query choices with responsibility maps. Explain semantic obligations, mutable structures, invariants, invalidation, coordination and recovery, including machinery absent from each path. Code size alone cannot measure necessary complexity. Revisit this comparison whenever a new mechanism could change its result.

### 13. Can the recommendation survive its strongest challenge? — S11

**Freeze policies before choosing held-out sources and parameter regions.** Challenge each proposed advantage with low reuse, adverse work placement, output pressure, constrained memory and sources just outside eligibility. Include ordinary no-choice work, incremental joins, partial information, substantive search, reconvergence and connected effects. Application examples can contribute without defining the language's domain.

**Audit each of the 57 questions and every distinct mechanism inside it.** Link the exact claim, evidence, contrary cases, remaining uncertainty and architectural consequence. Demonstrate why the strongest feasible follow-up could no longer change the bounded decision before treating it as resolved. A tradeoff can be the result; invented workload weights cannot turn it into a universal winner.

## How the sequence reaches every unanswered direction

**Review selection after every experimental package and review the entire portfolio after at most four.** Correctness, attribution and measurement qualification each count as packages. At the next review, include unfinished lifecycle studies as well as unimplemented mechanisms; otherwise a sequence can collect many source gates without answering whether any candidate pays.

For each next-package proposal, record the decision it could change, the strongest ready distinct alternative, actual prerequisites, expected implementation/measurement effort, and the next result boundary. Prefer completing a consequential comparison over expanding its matrix when the expansion cannot change the decision. Prefer a distinct mechanism over another refinement when the refinement has no credible path to changing the architecture comparison.

**The current handoff is to search restoration, with unfinished symbolic, demand and relevant-key work reconsidered at its next decision boundary.** Each study may need multiple bounded packages, but a fourth-package review cannot merely reset the counter. Further depth requires a written comparison with the next distinct experiment. At every such review, account for all rows in the order table and retain a concrete next action for unfinished work. Changing order supplies no evidence against the displaced design.

**A thorough trial requires six kinds of evidence.** Show the distinctive mechanism running; establish a competent implementation; measure full costs against strong controls; exercise favorable and adverse regimes; account for source and language boundaries; and challenge the resulting architectural inference. An analytical impossibility or demonstrated equivalence can replace an applicable experiment, provided its premises cover the actual proposal.

## Registration and interpretation

**This sequence fixes the required contrasts; each executable package needs a prospective registration.** After correctness and explicitly exploratory sizing, freeze exact source files, configurations, seeds, controls, oracle, endpoints, toolchain, binary and runner. Register warmups, randomized or interleaved paired blocks, repetitions, resource limits, exclusion rules, analysis and the practical difference that could affect the decision. Do not invent executable matrix sizes for a mechanism that has not yet been implemented and qualified.

Start sizing with a 60-second wall/CPU and 1-GiB per-process limit where compatible, plus a finite service/output bound. Justify exceptions before running. Use at least five primary repetitions per cell for sizing and two independent diagnostic repeats. Choose adequate confirmatory repetitions from observed variability before confirmation, rather than treating five samples as an architectural verdict. A limit reached calls for diagnosis, a justified extension or a sound bound on the unresolved claim.

Use ordinary-allocator counter-free primary timing, with work and allocation diagnostics in separate builds. Reuse prepared rules across changing queries. Charge preparation, query setup, execution, first/full observation, cancellation and disposal, plus compilation and artifacts for compilation claims. Validate complete independent answers outside measured intervals where possible; disclose inseparable phases and harness interference. Keep requested allocation, live heap, virtual reservations and RSS distinct. Preserve reference independence.

| Loss dominated by a consequential avoidable cost | Attribute and repair it, then repeat the affected comparison against the same competent controls. |

## What finishes the research

The final deliverable is an evidence-backed comparison of complete architectures, with a justified disposition for every relevant direction. Each disposition gives the tested claim, favorable and contrary evidence, total-cost and complexity consequences, language scope, and the strongest remaining challenge.

An unfinished mechanism or consequential unmeasured variant remains required work. An exact external blocker or necessary language-adoption decision must be named with its consequences and all independent work completed. Lower priority, implementation effort, a completed pilot and a completed sequence document cannot establish that the research goal is finished.
