# Experimental sequence for the unanswered architecture questions

Investigate the distinct alternatives, give each a credible trial, and compare the complete architectures they enable. Use the existing baselines as controls. Completing a pilot, or this sequence document, does not complete the research goal.

The central correction is to separate **priority from resolution**. Decision value determines which question to investigate first. A direction receives a resolved conclusion only from applicable evidence; implementation difficulty, a neighboring prototype's loss, and a preference for the current architecture do not supply that evidence.

This document owns the execution order. The [governing sequence](sequence.md) supplies detailed stage specifications and measurement rules. The [57-question map](question-to-experiment-map.md) assigns every reviewed question; the [mechanism ledger](remaining-investigations.md#distinctions-that-must-not-disappear-inside-a-stage) distinguishes proposals grouped within those questions. Those obligations all remain in force.

## Start here: the next experiments

**Finish the demand resource-dependency correctness gate already in progress, then investigate contextual equality and local consuming execution.** The current worktree contains an experimental implementation and registration extending the [demand resource study](results/S03-demand-resource.md); their presence is not a validated architectural result. Audit its source and receipts before relying on it. T071 remains the active experimental task.

The first cycle has four packages, each with a specific question:

| Package | Experiment and controls | Decision and next action |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Finish the source gate | Force an unresolved call output used as a passive resource key. Compare independent scalar answers and applicable compiled controls across aliases, constructors, competing consumers, alternatives, failure and resource-mediated recursion. Check that forcing a producer cannot permit a stale resource claim. | Can demand execution support this dependency without changing consumption or equality? A failure requires distinguishing a defect from a semantic obstruction. Success establishes this capability only. Nonground body posts and arbitrary writable heads remain separate questions. |
| 2. Attribute the dependency cost | Compare known and delayed keys, shallow and nested dependencies, and useful versus fruitless forcing. Reuse prepared rules across changing queries; check cancellation and held answers. Use existing competent execution controls, separate work/allocation diagnostics and counter-free lifecycle timing after sizing. | Does the added dependency mechanism have a consequential cost, and which responsibility causes it? Repair only costs capable of changing this comparison. If the implementation cannot yet support a credible comparison, record the exact prerequisite and reconsider the next package immediately. |
| 3. Establish contextual integration | Run an equality-enabled consuming rule in contextual equality and local rewriting organizations, against dedicated equality and current integrated graph scanning. Include shared deductions across contexts, incompatible contexts, broad aliases and positive guards. Show an actual boundary or repeated deduction disappearing. | Does integration save different work from immutable-term sharing or branch copying? Flat relations, CHR-expressed merging and strategic local rewrites need separate witnesses where their obligations differ; an operation/ownership equivalence can combine genuinely equivalent variants. |
| 4. Test the consequential integration contrast | Qualify the surviving source paths, then compare useful shared deductions with sparse-use, broad-repair and mostly-distinct-term controls. Charge discovery, repair, preparation, observation and disposal. | Does the benefit repay the organization it requires? An allocation loss is not a runtime verdict. Attribute a consequential loss; challenge a gain. If package 3 exposes a prerequisite, use this package to resolve it rather than timing an invalid path. |

**Why start here?** The dependency gate is already implemented far enough to make its audit a low-cost capability question. Its answer affects eligibility of a substantially different executor. Contextual integration comes next because sharing deductions and eliminating service boundaries could change the representation itself; further tuning of one measured mechanism cannot answer that question.

**The strongest ready alternative is joint symbolic solving: matched runtime, compiled formula reuse, identity lifetime and symbolic union.** The [joint ownership result](results/S06-joint-ownership.md) establishes an adverse allocation case that identity-allocation repair alone cannot reverse. It does not establish runtime inferiority or settle richer symbolic operations. Joint solving has the next position after this cycle unless the package reviews identify a stronger decision consequence. It remains required work.

Each package ends with a selection review. Four packages are a maximum before a full breadth review, not permission to ignore contrary evidence until then. Count correctness, qualification and attribution separately. A changed order records the affected decision, actual prerequisite, expected effort and explicit return point for displaced work.

**Language design is a comparison within these investigations, not an afterthought.** For ownership, linearity, modes, groundness, nonoverlap, finite domains and progress properties, compare inference, checked declarations and mandatory restrictions where they differ. Show an executable beneficiary, accepted and excluded programs, checker misses, concrete reformulations and duties eliminated. A restriction is useful only if its benefit and expressive cost are visible. This covers review entries 21, 30, 32–34 and 51–54.

**Changed meaning must be evaluated explicitly.** Compare stable positive guards with state-inspecting guards, logical sets with consuming multisets, and raw answers with projected observations. Show programs that distinguish the contracts and the implementation consequences. An experiment may inform a language choice; it cannot silently adopt that choice to make a candidate faster.

**Lifetime and architecture composition start early.** Each candidate carries ownership, cancellation and complete-answer checks from its first cost trial. Rows 7 and 9 consolidate and extend those obligations across candidates; they are not prerequisites for noticing retention or interaction costs. Native feasibility can also start before the preceding rows finish.

## How the sequence reaches every unanswered direction

**Coverage is checked at the mechanism level.** The [57-question map](question-to-experiment-map.md) provides the entry-by-entry assignments; the [mechanism ledger](remaining-investigations.md#distinctions-that-must-not-disappear-inside-a-stage) prevents distinct designs from disappearing inside broad labels. At every four-package review, list each still-untried mechanism, its first feasible experiment, its prerequisite and the decision it could change. Identify the next selected package and a return point for every displaced scheduled investigation.

**A thorough trial answers six questions.** Did the distinctive mechanism actually operate? Was the implementation credible? Did it get both a favorable opportunity and an adverse challenge? Were complete costs and language obligations exposed? Does the conclusion survive its strongest plausible objection? Which consequential variants still lack evidence? The [governing criteria](sequence.md#what-counts-as-investigating-a-design-thoroughly) apply to every row.

Use four evidence statuses: **not yet tested**, **implementation or measurement in progress**, **bounded result with remaining questions**, and **resolved for a stated claim**. Scheduling a question later changes neither its evidence nor its completion status. A resource cutoff records unfinished work; a correctness gate supplies no performance conclusion.

## Registration and interpretation

**Register confirmation after exploratory sizing and before comparative runs.** Each registration fixes the hypothesis, exact sources/configurations/seeds, controls, independent oracle, observation and scheduling contract, repetitions, warmups, randomized order, practical threshold, resource limits and analysis. Freeze source, toolchain, binary and runner identities. The [measurement rules](sequence.md#registration-bounds-and-interpretation) govern every cost study.

Use ordinary-allocator counter-free primary timing; run work counters and requested-heap diagnostics separately. Begin sizing with a 60-second and 1-GiB per-process bound where compatible, plus a finite service/output bound. Justify exceptions prospectively. Start with at least five primary repetitions per cell and two independent diagnostic repeats; use sizing variability to register an adequate confirmation count before examining confirmatory outcomes. These bounds control experimental cost, not architectural eligibility.

Charge preparation, changing-query setup, execution, first/full observation, cancellation and disposal. Reuse prepared rules across changing queries. Compilation claims also require compilation, host work and artifacts. Validate complete answers independently outside timing intervals. Disclose inseparable phases and harness overhead; keep requested bytes, live heap, virtual reservations and RSS distinct. Preserve reference-interpreter independence.

| Outcome | Required response |
|---|---|
| Incorrect behavior | Separate a defective implementation from an obstruction under the stated semantics. Repair defects; scope counterexamples to their premises. |
| Loss without the promised benefit occurring | Construct a favorable source where the mechanism operates, or demonstrate why that benefit cannot occur. |
| Loss dominated by a correctable cost | Attribute it and repair it when it could reverse the decision. A competent alternative is necessary for architectural rejection. |
| Gain | Challenge low reuse, overhead, adverse placement, sustained lifetime and source restrictions. |
| Different favorable regimes | Investigate a consequential crossover and the cost of recognizing it. Report conditional tradeoffs without invented weights. |
| Overlap or cutoff | Diagnose measurement uncertainty or unfinished work. Seek further evidence when the answer could affect the architecture. |
| Further precision cannot affect the decision | Record the sensitivity argument and advance to another question; retain any broader unanswered variants. |

## What finishes the research

The final deliverable is an evidence-backed architectural comparison with a justified disposition for every relevant unresolved direction. It explains total efficiency, required responsibilities and invariants, source coverage and language tradeoffs. It links favorable results, contrary cases, raw receipts and the scope of every conclusion.

A precise counterexample or demonstrated equivalence can resolve an applicable claim without another implementation. A credible bounded comparison can justify a bounded recommendation. An unfinished candidate, an unmeasured consequential variant, or a decision to investigate later leaves required work. An external or owner-dependent decision must be named exactly; it does not become experimental evidence against a design.

The research goal stays active while feasible investigation is likely to materially improve the architectural decision relative to its cost. Completing this programme's first pass is a review point, not an automatic stopping rule.
