## Inference and interpretation

For 1,944 contrasts, per-contrast error is 0.05/1944. With 64 ratios, choose the largest integer k such that twice the fair-binomial tail through k-1 is at most that error. This gives k=16: interval endpoints are sorted ratios 16 and 49 (one-based). The fair-binomial tail bounds excursions of an interval past the median under independent repetitions from a stable distribution; Bonferroni bounds family error by 5% without assuming independence between contrasts. Ties are conservative. Temporal dependence or host nonstationarity can invalidate the sampling model. These are conditional machine/run-series intervals, not guarantees across hardware or source programs.
