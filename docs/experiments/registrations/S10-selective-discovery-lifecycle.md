# S10 selective discovery: registered lifecycle pilot

Registered before comparative runs. Test whether the candidate reduction in the source/work gate repays filtered-list construction, retention and disposal. Existing conditional execution is the paired implementation control; existing Scan and source-derived counting are stronger complete-path controls. No default or architecture is selected.

## Hypotheses and configurations

H1: filtering consumed occurrences and immutable constructor mismatches can reduce total requested traffic and peak on recursive and branch-specific work. H2: dense heads, shallow work and early failure can expose list-building overhead even with fewer candidate entries. H3: counting away common traversal can change the usefulness of discovery filtering; component gains must not be added together.

Use three executors: existing conditional, filtered conditional (`selective-discovery` build), and Scan. Run both original and source-counted inputs for common, independent-suffix and early-failure families. Cross choices 0/3, depth 0/16, reuse 1/4 and eligible/missing-permit schedules. Missing permits occur on odd query indices, retain ordinary execution when counting declines and preserve complete suspended answers. This gives 288 cells.

Add dense propagation over one and six occurrences, original source only, reuse 1/4, each of the three executors: 12 cells. The six-occurrence source requires all 30 ordered distinct pairs and their residual observations. Its source has no private traversal to count. Depth is fixed at zero; the runner's choice-count selector 0/3 denotes one/six occurrences for this family, not source choices. All queries change their atom names across reuse.

Total 300 cells. Two isolated allocation processes per cell (600), exact replay of every phase's memory reading, shuffled seed 7802. Only after that gate, five ordinary timing processes per cell (1500), shuffled seed 7803. Total 2100 processes. Freeze four binaries (existing/filtered × meter/time), source/registration/driver hashes, toolchain and execution orders. Scan runs in the existing binary and is not duplicated in the filtered binary.

## Bounds and measurements

Per process: 1 GiB address space, 60 CPU seconds, 75 wall seconds. Each finite query is bounded by 500,000 public service calls. Cutoffs or failures require diagnosis and do not count as losses or empty answers. Poll any live run; do not restart it after an observation timeout.

Use the counter-free native lifecycle runner. Engine/kernel counters remain disabled in both builds. Allocation runs use the requested-allocation meter; primary time uses the ordinary allocator. Allocation is requested heap, not RSS. Five timing observations yield exploratory medians/ranges, not formal practical-win claims.

Measure counting inference, preparation, original input construction, transformation, search setup, first complete delivery/exhaustion, remaining complete delivery, search disposal, answer disposal, transformed/input disposal separately. Reuse source preparation and counting certificate across changed queries. Include one public-service cancellation probe and all remaining disposal. Cancellation uses the first missing-permit query for that schedule. Every query/cancellation must restore the prepared baseline; dropping preparation and inference must restore their respective prior baselines. Every adjacent phase must agree on live ownership.

Warm and measured full answers must agree with independently constructed expected observations; the independent scalar must agree outside intervals. Validate optimizer admission counts and build identity independently. Dense sources reject inference and are never counted. Exclude original source AST construction before inference, native compilation, process startup, validation and preallocated bookkeeping. Execution and observation are coupled in delivery. Do not claim compilation-inclusive superiority or sustained-lifetime coverage.

## Interpretation and decision

Report matched cells and phase attribution, with both favorable and adverse results. Do not average across families, invent workload weights or infer an architecture winner from tuple counts. A lower traffic result can coexist with a higher peak or slower time. Diagnose consequential defects or large construction costs before attributing a loss to conditional representation itself.

After this pilot, compare the decision value of a measured filter repair against resumable contextual discovery. Further refinement requires a consequential identified cost; otherwise carry the bounded evidence forward. Broader support-aware joining, representation choices, language contracts, sustained lifetime and coherent architecture comparison remain required.
