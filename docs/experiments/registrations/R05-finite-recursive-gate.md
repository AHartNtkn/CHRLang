- One head has a nullary constructor in an inferred control position; the other has a unary constructor with a variable child there. Each other position is a distinct variable, also distinct from the step child. These cases are disjoint and exhaustive over the admitted finite spine domain.
- Flatten conjunctions in order. Base effects are equations or true. Step effects are equations or true followed by exactly one call to the same predicate; its control argument is the matched child variable. There is no work after that call, explicit failure goal, choice, external constraint, base recursion or additional recursive call.

## Correspondence argument to audit

At every step there is one source occurrence. Its ground control enables exactly one nonbinding head case; distinct variable head positions impose no further tests. Body equations use a fresh scope for each application. Their finite-tree solutions either fail or preserve the ground control child. The recursive call therefore decreases the known finite spine depth. Base equations terminate. There is no alternative history, propagation or residual constraint on success.

By induction on control depth, replacing the sole source application with its ordered equations and next argument tuple preserves finite failure or the most-general finite-tree substitution projected jointly onto query outputs. Fresh existential variable names are observationally irrelevant but aliases are not. Success has exactly one raw answer and empty residual; failure has zero. The direct loop may reuse a candidate finite-tree term kernel, but its source correspondence must use an independent scalar source evaluator and separate mathematical expectations, never its own execution as the oracle.

This argument does not permit moving equations before an interfering outside rule. Existing R05 binding/observation/divergence counterexamples remain relevant exclusions. Termination is established by the certificate, not by a test cutoff.
