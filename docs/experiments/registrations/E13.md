# E13 finite specialization

Status: first semantic and implementation gates; no performance matrix frozen.
The reference remains independent. Earlier E12 refinements are still open.

## First correspondence boundary

Begin with a closed single-active-call fragment: each reachable active predicate
has exactly one unguarded simplification rule with one linear variable-only head.
Each body path has at most one active call, in tail position after its equations
and passive posts. The query has at most one active call. A predicate with no
matching rule is passive and its occurrences remain in the output. Reachability
includes every body arm. A second consumer, multihead rule, propagation rule,
guard, constructor head or non-tail active call makes this certificate fail.
Unreachable rules do not interfere unless one of their heads consumes a reachable
predicate. This is experimental compiler eligibility, not an adopted language ban.

This boundary permits a direct execution-tree argument: freshening and a unique
catchall expansion replace a finite single-active-call prefix; retained equations
publish the same bindings, explicit OR retains each alternative, and a residual
call retains recursion when the compilation budget ends. There is no other active
constraint whose selection boundary can be crossed. Finite successful derivations
and full residuals correspond up to fresh-variable renaming. Bounded prefix
compression may change answer latency/order; it does not imply a fixed source-step
trace or a fairness proof for arbitrary CHR.

The single-active premise is material. With p <=> p and q <=> fail, a committed
selector can repeatedly choose p while q remains pending in the store. Inlining q's
failure into the caller's pending body can instead terminate that branch. Add an
executable counterexample before treating general closed logical inlining as
operationally equivalent. Multi-call specialization remains a separate feasible
investigation: preserve source boundaries, establish a stronger commutation
certificate, or explicitly study a different operational contract. Do not close
it merely because the first fragment is ready.

## First compiler

Specialize the query's active entry call using its known finite term skeleton and
aliases. Add one private entry rule whose head preserves that skeleton; freshen
all unfolded body variables. Retain original definitions for residual calls.
Use a global finite expansion budget and keep the original call when it is spent.
Preserve every explicit OR. Track compile-time substitutions separately in each
arm; do not propagate one arm's bindings to its sibling or assume a common join.

Keep runtime equations that bind caller holes, even when their consequences are
used to specialize later work. Elide only entailed identity equations; replace a
provably inconsistent equation with failure. Keep active failing or recursive
calls regardless of whether their outputs are requested. Record expansions,
proved contradictions, elided equations and generated goal size. A finite variant
policy beyond the single entry is a distinct follow-up.

Validate arithmetic in every registered direction, repeated variables and output
shapes at budgets 0,1,2,4,8. In particular add(X,Y,s(B)) must keep its zero arm.
Test unknown arguments, passive residual aliases, explicit duplicate alternatives,
unused failure/divergence, and rejection of intercepting/non-tail rules. Compare
compiled execution with independently registered observations and reference runs;
no speed conclusion precedes a credible scalar comparison including compilation.

## Initial eligibility/observation inventory

Run all 64 E00 cases at expansion budget eight. Record every eligibility reason,
compiled size counters and reference execution comparison for eligible inputs.
For finite reference cases require full expected observations, raw successes and
exhaustion. For prefix cases report those comparisons without treating matching
a prefix as exhaustive coverage or making an answer-order contract. This is an
exploratory compiler coverage inventory, not a comparative performance matrix.
Rejected application families remain research obligations for broader certificates
or preservation of source scheduling boundaries.

The scheduling counterexample concerns termination under the fixed experimental
selector. It does not refute finite-answer equivalence of closed logical programs
or validity under other permitted ordinary CHR schedules. A broader compiler may
be studied with an explicitly different permitted schedule. The tail certificate
must not become an inferred global language restriction or a reason to abandon
multi-call and synthesis specialization.
