# Adaptive search lifecycle timing pilot

Prospective exploratory registration before implementation or comparative timing. T077 asks whether changed independence checking, execution and allocation improve complete costs against credible explicit controls. The stateless-eager gate and ownership matrix are the first two packages after the projection breadth review. Runner/endpoint qualification is package three; this timing pilot is package four and must end with full breadth review.

## Hypotheses and controls

Use the qualified Copy, initial-phase reunion, stateless eager, scheduled EveryBoundary, fixed skip 1/8 and failed-check backoff cap 1/8 paths. Keep their static engine types in the runner, rather than padding eager with a largest-variant wrapper. No policy changes or tuning during this pilot.

H1: backoff can repay skipped checks in late-link sources, but Copy or fixed schedules may remain cheaper. H2: history sources with successful checks give backoff no saved-check benefit and expose its bookkeeping. H3: private work and preparation reuse can change whether repeated separation earns its costs. H4: cancellation and retained outputs can change the ordering. A memory reduction does not predict a timing win; a source-work reduction does not include checking, transport or output.

## Exact matrix

Plain/history/late sources from the existing repeated-source fixture; three additional rounds; private depths 0 and 4; one or four changing query identities; complete exhaustion or cancellation after the first permitted answer; immediate release or retaining all answers across the batch. Eight modes × three families × two depths × two reuse counts × two completion choices × two retention choices = 384 cells. Execute five ordinary-allocator metrics-off release processes and two independent allocation-meter processes per cell: 2,688 pilot processes. Retained windows already have owner qualification; this pilot measures their two endpoint extremes without claiming a general lifetime result.

Before the matrix, qualify the exact runner on all 384 cells once in each build (768 bounded endpoint/owner checks). These observations are qualification, not samples in the five-repetition pilot. Require no process over ten seconds before retaining the matrix size. Freeze sources, binaries, commands and environment before qualification; freeze the shuffled pilot schedule with seed 770903 before comparative runs. Each process has 60 seconds wall/CPU, 1 GiB address space and 200,000 service calls per query. Preserve failures and cutoffs; do not rank incomplete runs.

## Validation and measurement boundaries

Independently compute complete answers and validate each candidate's full preflight before measurement, using the unchanged scalar evaluator and exact alias/resource/multiplicity comparison. Preflight includes the same query identities. During measurement each output is passed through black_box before release/retention; require exact expected output counts and exhaustion or the requested first-answer stop. Retained measured answers receive independent complete/membership validation after producer disposal. Immediate-release results cannot be re-read after disposal: their complete semantic evidence is the identical-source preflight and existing owned-output gate, not a count-only proof of general correctness. No output hashing, canonicalization or oracle work is charged as engine execution.

Measure source construction, preparation/eligibility, source disposal, changing query input, engine setup, first answer including owned materialization, remaining execution/observation, engine disposal, input disposal, prepared disposal and consumer disposal. Immediate answer disposal occurs inside delivery; retained-all disposal has its own phase. Source rules are created inside measurement and dropped after preparation. Independent expected outputs and preallocated measurement bookkeeping remain outside. The preparation must own what the engines need. After preparation disposal retained answers remain valid; after consumer disposal measured live heap must return exactly to baseline in allocation builds.

The source-to-first-answer time includes construction, preparation and query setup. Also report warm-query first-answer costs separately. Full lifecycle is the sum of nonoverlapping charged phases. The first-answer call is separated by a clock boundary from the remaining service; validation does not interrupt measured service between subsequent answers. Each process preflights before measuring, so this is warmed in-process service, not cold process startup. Compilation, artifacts and RSS are not isolated here and support no architectural lifecycle-superiority claim. Operational service/output bounds remain enabled; diagnostic engine/kernel counters are disabled in primary builds. Metered timing is not primary evidence.

## Interpretation and next decision

Report all five timing values, their range and median, phase medians, and exact allocation pairs. Flag an exploratory gain only when the candidate's maximum lifecycle time is below 90% of the control's minimum; flag loss when its minimum exceeds 110% of the control's maximum; otherwise unresolved. This is a screening rule, not a confidence interval. Retain Copy and fixed schedules in every inference about backoff, and distinguish stateless eager from scheduled EveryBoundary. Do not count policy bookkeeping as a separate source capability.

If a measurement or owner fails, investigate before continuing. Attribute a consequential loss to the responsible phases and examine whether a feasible repair could reverse the architectural consequence. A favorable policy needs new source/parameter challenges before broader adoption; two depths are not a universal workload model. Do not invent weights. At the full breadth review compare further adaptive work with incremental projection, native local ownership, conditional equality/lifetime, broader caller reuse and each direction still missing a credible trial. Keep delayed splitting, successful-but-unprofitable separation, checkpoints/replay and broader independence inference required.
