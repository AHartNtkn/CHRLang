# Learned failed-domain regions: source and validity gate

Test a bounded form of compatible-query learning: a completed failed finite phase proves that its whole input-domain region is infeasible. Reuse that region to exclude only the covered assignments of a later, possibly wider successful query. This differs from an exact whole-query result-cache hit. It is not yet conflict analysis from failed subderivations inside a successful query.

Scope learning to one immutable Prepared finite-phase owner. Key by the ordered private goals after producer extraction, normalized for query-variable renaming. Every private variable must have an initial finite producer domain. Caller state and nonempty independent producer domains may differ; they cannot affect the checked private prefix before its completion. Private-goal changes and changed alias topology must miss. Domain weights affect successful derivation counts but not infeasibility; exclusions retain the later query's weights. Ground producer failure at entry supplies no generalizable proof.

Only complete zero-solution reports may install a learned region. Cancellation, suspended phases and resource errors must not learn failure. Later domains may overlap, extend or avoid a region; subtract intersections into disjoint residual domain boxes, preserving raw multiplicity. Bound retained region count; eviction affects reuse opportunity only. Charge exclusion-created partitions against the existing limit, and report probes/exclusions separately from actual solver steps.

Test successful wider queries after failed seeds, renamed variables, changed domain supports and duplicate weights, changed aliases, changed private goals, irrelevant caller constraints, ordinary token consumption and resumed full callers. Compare recomputation and an executable exact-query result cache separately. Independently enumerate small sources through the reference/scalar semantics and existing compiled control; compare complete raw answers, aliases and residual multiplicity. Include late failure after deterministic private calls, overlapping rule priorities, invalid admission, cancellation, step/partition limits and zero-capacity retention.

A favorable witness must actually skip solver work in a successful changed query, not merely return a cached empty result. An adverse witness must show no reuse under incompatible goals or disjoint domains. Record all fixed parameter combinations before the final gate. No timing or allocation advantage is claimed by this source gate. At its boundary compare further learning work with T078 allocation qualification and coherent-path costs; broader conflict learning, resources and native compilation remain required.
