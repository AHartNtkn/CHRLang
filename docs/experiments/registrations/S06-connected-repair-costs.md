pilot with three configurations: repaired projection, frozen original projection,
and early-filtered enumeration. Verify the original binaries against the original
pilot manifest before use. New builds disable phase clocks and metrics; allocation
uses a separate alloc-meter build. Keep source, input, preparation, observation,
retention, cancellation and disposal endpoints unchanged.

There are 576 cells: three configurations × three families × two sizes × duplicate
choices absent/present × output aliases absent/present × one/four queries × retain
none/all × complete/first tuple. Run two exact ownership repetitions per cell,
then ten shuffled ordinary blocks (seed 607093): 1,152 allocation and 5,760 timing
processes. Every process retains the existing complete independent preflight,
measured record checks and root ownership conservation. Consumer bytes must match
all three configurations. Bounds remain 1 GiB address space, 30 CPU seconds,
45 wall seconds, 100,000 projection operations and 5,000,000 enumeration steps.

phase sums, median paired ratio and all ten block signs: gain median <=0.90 and
all <1, loss median >=1.10 and all >1, otherwise unresolved. Require both medians
above the largest registered per-process clock floor (100 × median empty clock
interval). Do not exclude contrary observations. Meter times are not evidence.

unequal-name stars, reducing full cost there; equality sources should expose the
remaining preparation/elimination costs. A component improvement does not imply

and choose between sparse elimination, ordered transport, call-boundary recognition
and the strongest other ready direction by decision value relative to cost.
