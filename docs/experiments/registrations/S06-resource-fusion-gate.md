# Source-derived fusion with counted resource availability

Investigate eliminating an effectful producer/consumer boundary, beyond pure-prefix inlining and a single recursive carrier. Infer a private intermediate from source: one producer posts only that intermediate after consuming its heads; an earlier consumer uniquely consumes the intermediate plus additional key-only resources. Both are simplifications without guards. Producer heads have a common key variable and otherwise distinct variable parameters; the intermediate transports bound variables. Consumer intermediate parameters are distinct variables. Predicate/variable names and payloads come from source, not workload names.

Controlled producer/resource predicates must have no other head owners or body producers; the intermediate has exactly the identified producer and consumer. Consumer output/equality/choice effects remain source-derived. Producer and extra-resource predicates are disjoint. This is a sufficient finite-resource fragment, not a language restriction.

For each changed query, reject initial intermediate occurrences and nonground controlled keys. Count available occurrences by ground key and predicate/arity. Bound producer firings by the minimum available producer-head count divided by its multiplicity, and require every extra-resource count to cover that bound times its consumer multiplicity. Insufficient resources must reject fusion: the original can otherwise consume producer inputs and leave an intermediate. Preserve spare resources and duplicate occurrences. This certificate checks resources, not execution traces.

Fuse the consumer body into the producer and append the extra consuming heads, using capture-free source substitution. Emit the fused producer and remaining nonconsumer rules in source order. Verify complete raw answers against independent scalar evaluation before/after and conventional generic/specialized Scan controls. Trace actual fewer source applications; preparation must not execute the original source.

Test renamed predicates/variables, multiple consumed resources, duplicate counts, changed queries, payload aliases, finite duplicate alternatives, fresh variables and ordinary remaining observers of final outputs. Include insufficient resource, nonground-key, intermediate-observer and competing-resource near misses. Bound finite source evaluation at200000 calls and variable identity arithmetic explicitly. No comparative native timings/allocations in this gate. At the boundary reconsider full lifecycle, native integrated matching, adaptive reunion and coherent architecture composition before extending the compiler fragment.
