## Exact comparison

`specialized` with no extra features, and `carrier` with `carrier-contraction`.
Specialized retains inferred specialization, Global selection and indexed access.
Carrier alone enables candidate fields and hooks. No COW comparison: T054's16
paired ranges overlap, and storage is not this question. This omission makes no
general claim about COW.

Unknown-tail cells have pre0,1,64 and post0, each q1,4. Query i adds i%2 to pre
and retains post0; therefore q4 alternates0/1,1/2,64/65 respectively. Ground controls
have pre64/post0,q1,4; q4 alternates(64,0)/(65,1). The existing independent full
answer oracle requires16 unique raw tuple assignments, aliases and residuals,
including distinct unknown tail/payload variables. The semantic gate separately
covers malformed tails and later bindings; no cost conclusion for those regimes.
