# Source-derived elimination of a finite private equation prefix

Test a transformation that removes eligible source occurrences and their matching, rather than recognizing an exact workload schema. Infer a leading source-rule prefix with one unconditionally matching removed head per rule, distinct variable parameters, no kept heads/guards, and no other head use of each private predicate. Its bodies may contain equations, finite explicit choices, true/fail and calls within the prefix; the call graph must be acyclic. Require source-order policy: every private rule precedes remaining resource rules.

Inline that prefix in remaining bodies and initial queries with capture-free fresh variables. Emit a finite kickoff body for the transformed initial query, preserving its variables and output aliases. Remaining resource rules retain their relative order and ordinary execution. This is source transformation into CHR, not a general direct solver, and it does not eliminate required unification, choices or consuming execution. Compilation/code growth and kickoff costs remain unmeasured.

Validate renamings, changed constants, nested private calls, fresh variables, duplicate choices and resource effects against independent source answers. Include near misses with an external observer of the private occurrence, constructor-dependent/late matching, recursion and a consuming rule before the candidate prefix. Demonstrate why moving binding before consumption is unsafe without the priority condition. Include a finite sibling beside an ordinary continuing branch. No timings are registered.
