# Stronger compiled controls: prospective complete lifecycle pilot

Compare qualified specialization, fresh prefix lowering and reusable prefix artifacts with conditional and explicit controls. Hypotheses: specialization may reduce complete cost despite unchanged alternatives; prefix inlining may lose through entry construction; reusable artifacts may recover that cost only within stable signatures. The conditional favorable regime may survive or disappear against these stronger controls. No ordering is assumed.

Same-signature queries keep predicate order/count and depth, but alternate work4/0 and payload8/0. Arrival residual tags alternate values without changing their position. Changing-signature queries retain work4/payload8, increase depth n..n+3 and alternate input order. Preparation is lazy: first-use artifact construction is charged in setup. Four genuinely distinct arrival signatures retain four artifacts; four same-signature queries retain one. Artifact-cache lookup/key construction is charged on every call. No source knowledge is preloaded outside measured phases.

Randomize within each kind; execute processes sequentially. Each process first validates full answers with analytical and independent scalar replay, using separate prepared owners. Timings are warm-within-process lifecycle observations, not cold-cache measurements. No further warmups.

## Lifetime and validation

Include first observation as a subinterval only. Fixed harness buffers, correctness replay, process startup and native compilation are excluded. No compilation-inclusive architecture claim.
