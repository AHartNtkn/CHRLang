## How this map controls the experiment sequence

**A mechanism gate establishes that the proposed work actually happens and remains correct.** It does not establish a cost advantage. Follow it with exploratory sizing, a prospectively registered comparison and investigation of consequential defects or contrary results.

**An architectural conclusion requires complete costs and a credible alternative.** Use ordinary-allocator counter-free timing, separate work/allocation diagnostics, independently validated complete answers, preparation reuse over changing queries, observation, cancellation and disposal. Compilation claims also require compilation and artifact costs. The [measurement rules](sequence.md#registration-bounds-and-interpretation) define repetitions, bounds and interpretation.

**A completed bounded comparison does not erase its unanswered variants.** Attach each result to the exact row proposition it tests, state its favorable and adverse regimes, and name what could reverse its consequence. The [decision record requirements](remaining-investigations.md#evidence-required-before-a-question-can-leave-the-sequence) govern when a question can leave the sequence.

**Priority determines order, not whether a question deserves evidence.** At every package boundary compare the next proposal with the strongest ready distinct investigation; review breadth after four packages, including correctness and attribution work. Continue useful depth when justified, with an explicit reason why it is more consequential than that alternative.
